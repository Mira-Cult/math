# Development and remaining work

This is a history of proof sources, not a claim of publication priority. The two older public gists, the October 19–20 sources, and later composition work have distinct statements and review scopes.

## 1. Older logarithm tables and sequential reading

The [earlier public archive](https://gist.github.com/Mira-acc/ea2791119de611279237096e35cbc976/d699a69c2037ee4420bfb8e1e0ad0b310bbc8eb0) contains a broad concatenation theory: entropy and collision-energy criteria, polynomial sampling, arithmetic streams, logarithm-table dovetails, adaptive absolute-normal reading, and strict sequential schedules. The logarithm-dovetail sources are OLD-S11–OLD-S15, at physical LF lines 5401–7016 of that revision. They distinguish adaptive reading from a stricter schedule that admits and reads rows in order.

These archived producer arguments are not all freshly audited by this release. In particular, an adaptive absolutely normal construction is not interchangeable with a strict sequential whole-prefix construction, and a countable artificial stream is not a finite expression for an unchanged classical constant.

## 2. Vanishing logarithmic perturbations and function theory

The [October 7 public archive](https://gist.github.com/Mira-acc/79be9a5d4aa54a9df41e702c4f3113ea/41b5a780f79f7bdd275cd96e7e310b000a897d35) contains eight conventional proof sources. The most relevant are:

- **S02, lines 3438–4661:** one logarithm per stage, with arbitrarily slowly divergent overhead above a logarithmic source count.
- **S03, lines 4674–6386:** uniform normality from vanishing logarithmic perturbations, common-kernel joining laws, and independent logarithmic families.
- **S04, lines 6399–7640:** function-transcendence obstructions for the declared architecture, normal Liouville repetition, and full-prefix interpolation rigidity.
- **S05, lines 7653–9083:** selected-argument approximation and analytic normal-value constructions. The bounded-density tail step in the nested-interval induction remains a priority for separate review.

The source schedule in S02/S03 uses complete prefixes of $\theta+\log(1+(bj+1)b^{-A_j})$, with an alignment condition on $A_j$ and a slowly varying repetition scale $v_j$. Its proof includes $S_J\sim v_JA_JD_J$ and $K_J/S_J\sim1/v_J$. The finite fresh-prime grid, the universal row-dilution obstruction, and the actual-start shadowing criterion are reproduced with proofs in [Transfer and obstructions](TRANSFER_AND_OBSTRUCTIONS.md).

The globally bounded-function obstruction in S04 is **schedule-specific**. Its arithmetic setting must not be converted into the assertion that rational or algebraic source functions can never yield a normal dovetail. The newer actual-derivative method supplies counterexamples to that overbroad interpretation.

The normal Liouville construction is classical background. A normal output can be made transcendental by separately imposing Liouville approximation, but normality itself supplies no transcendence theorem. The selected-input statements likewise do not prove a special value such as $\zeta(3)$ normal.

## 3. Constant-precision batches and actual derivatives

The October 19 multiscale work changed the proof mechanism while keeping the exact affine numerator and full copied prefixes. The fresh-prime CRT method provides specialized schedules for logarithm, four times arctangent, and a three-pole logarithmic primitive. The actual-derivative method avoids Taylor truncation and period completion, and admits every nonpolynomial rational derivative regular at zero.

Its separate quantitative research includes near-logarithmic copied length, prescribed-growth classifications for the three CRT kernels, moving windows, and family-specific discrepancy lower bounds. Those stronger resource profiles are not asserted for every function admitted by the newer coefficient-window theorem. The full resource-optimization theory deserves a separate public treatment; this release does not claim to have reviewed or reproduced all of it.

## 4. Real D-finite functions and parameterized operations

The corrected October 20 coefficient-window paper is the principal source of the [main manuscript](MANUSCRIPT.md). Its key observation is that an invertible polynomial-coefficient recurrence gives a factorial-scale lower bound on the norm of each coefficient window. This replaces rational-coefficient arithmetic by a reusable analytic hypothesis and allows a common computable schedule for an entire common-radius class.

The [extension drafts](EXTENSIONS.md) distinguish three phenomena:

1. Structural failure: an exact polynomial cancellation defeats every admissible schedule in the stated model.
2. Schedule suppression: a flat positive source can produce all-zero copied rows under one schedule.
3. Rescue by slower shrinkage: a different schedule can admit specified reciprocal-exponential flat functions.

The near-exception result concerns triangular comparisons between distinct fixed-parameter outputs; it does not allow the parameter to change from row to row within one stream. Compact-uniform singular-power control across positive integer parameters remains open.

## 5. Later theory not promoted by this release

On 11 November 2026, later source work remained in draft branches on position-aligned splicing, fractional schedules, and certified joint normality operations. It includes composition questions, shifted arithmetic channels, and explicit joint-family constructions. A separate Gaussian branch studies another setting. These are active research lines, not missing lemmas silently assumed by this manuscript.

Their proofs and exact-head reviews require separate publication decisions. Scalar marginal normality does not imply joint normality, and the present theorem gives no blanket closure under sums, products, exponentials, arbitrary analytic composition, or row-varying operations. Private draft links would not give a public reader a usable proof, so they are not used as supporting references here.

## 6. Concrete open questions

- Extend admission beyond the coefficient-window class without assuming nonexistent uniform nondegeneracy.
- Establish compact-uniform singular-power bounds through positive integer parameters, or identify a genuine obstruction.
- Classify copied length, precision, source-admission count, depth, and discrepancy jointly. Bounds from different schedules cannot simply be combined.
- Prove useful operation/composition rules with the full joint or conditional distribution information and every switching/carry cost.
- Find an independently meaningful target whose **shifted** orbit is shadowed by a constructed dovetail at the scale required by the transfer theorem.
- Complete a targeted literature and expert proof review before making a novelty claim or presenting the package as journal-ready.
