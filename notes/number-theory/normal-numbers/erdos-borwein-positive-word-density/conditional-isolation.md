# Conditional isolation of an exact divisor term

Fix a>7/12, and let H≥N^a, H≤N. Preserve the exact adaptive squarefree half-marker incidences (n,j0,b,p) and notation of [adaptive marker count](adaptive-marker-count.md). Ambient incidence mean λ=E_n F is comparable to θ, and E_n F²=O_a(θ) for all sufficiently small fixed θ. The law I is uniform over all genuine incidences, with no fitting of the actual row. We aim to show that on a positive fraction of I the selected term is exactly 1/2 and the complete complementary circle product is within chord distance 1 of 1. This would give positive ambient density for {01,10} by Cauchy–Schwarz, hence for each separately because switch-type counts differ by at most one on a contiguous interval of starts. End trim by one digit if required.

## 1. Parameters and polynomial terminal bank

Fix W large, L=exp(20W), B*=ceil(exp(50W)), Q the least even integer ≥100W, and H0=W^4. They are chosen before N; θ is small fixed as in the adaptive incidence theorem. Let t=loglog X=T+O(1) on fixed b, with X≍N/b. Short-BV has some positive modulus exponent ρ(a) (as sourced and paid in [marked tail](marked-tail.md)). Reserve less than ρ/4 for bank products across all Q+1 labels, and less than ρ/4 for at most two forced upper primes or powers. Use first bank endpoint X^(1/T^8). Force tests may overlap bank primes; on conditioning on each prescribed reduced class, the number of fixed prime increments changes by at most two per label. Later primes hit at most one label, as they exceed all shift differences. The first bank may use its larger fixed Q-dependent Taylor degree, which consumes o(1) modulus exponent as N grows.

For later natural stages use r_i=ceil(A L_(i−1)), A=C(Q+1)^2 with absolute sufficiently large C, and reversed geometric shares ≤ρ/(100(Q+1)) summing ≤ρ/(50(Q+1)); its final share before bridge/final bank is fixed ρ/(100(Q+1)). End the natural portion one step before the last L_(i−1)≥H0. Thus the last natural degree is ≍A exp(U), with U∈[H0,exp(H0)) that next/last natural height, and its cutoff exponent is ≍ρ/[(Q+1)A exp(U)]. For large N this natural portion is nonempty. Use fixed final degree r_f=ceil(10000 A H0) and final cutoff exponent c=ρ/[100(Q+1) r_f]. If U≤10^5 H0, go directly to that final bank; its new mean is O(U+log r_f) and r_f is at least a sufficiently large constant multiple of it by choice of A. If U>10^5H0, insert one bridge with degree r_B=ceil(AU) and cutoff exponent ρ/[1000(Q+1)A exp(H0)]. Its degree spends ≤ρ/[900(Q+1)] eventually, is ≥10r_f, and is much smaller than the preceding degree ≍A exp(U). Its mean is O(U), followed by final bank mean O(H0+log r_f). In both branches the endpoints are ordered, all mixed degrees with force tests fit ρ, and c^-1 is polynomial in W (with constants depending on a).

The quantitative error of the fixed-order marked LLT follows by the recursion in [weighted local comparison](weighted-local-comparison.md), used up to Q+1 labels, in particular exp(−r_f) times t^(-(q)/2) for a q-coordinate exact atom, plus exp(−κt). Here the later stages need only polynomial degree multiplier: at a prime later than the first bank, labelled hit outcomes are disjoint. Products of positive binomial factorials are bounded by products of powers of means divided by factorials, by dropping the inter-label distinctness restriction; deterministic increments from ≤2 forced primes can be included as Bernoulli trials of probability one. Thus for every nonempty endpoint subset each factor at degree r_i has bound (2e(μ_i+2)/r_i)^{r_i}≤e^(-8r_i), by the chosen A. The lower coefficient sum is ≤exp(2(Q+1)(μ_i+2)). The Q-dimensional max model atom is ≤C^(Q+1)t^(-(q)/2), using high primes and integration of separated Gaussian factors. Consequently the recursive relative-error prefactor is bounded by C^(Q+1) r_i^(Q+1) times these factors; successive degree gaps ≥10 and H0=W^4 suffice to propagate ε_i≤e^(-r_i). Additive BV errors still decay as exp(−κt): all later degrees sum O_W(log T), including the fixed bridge, and the first degree plus coefficient/modulus recovery costs exp(O_W(t)), absorbed by arbitrary fixed log savings. This justifies the quantitative polynomial terminal cutoff, not merely an adjustable fixed cutoff with unknown dependence on W.

## 2. Avoidance and its marked version

For all j≠j0, 0≤j≤J, let U_j be the terminal bank count on the stripped affine form (as in the size-biased note), and B_j=j+1−U_j. Let I_b be the central sites |j−T|≤L sqrt(T), j≠j0, restricted to [0,J]. For fixed L and large N this meets the stated range throughout its central part, since J≥10T. Define A_b by |B_j|>W at every j∈I_b. Let γ_t(v)=(2πt)^(-1/2) exp(−(v−t)²/(2t)), and put p_j=Σ_{|s|≤W} γ_t(j+1−s). Then Σ_{I_b}p_j→(2W+1)P(|G|≤L), G standard normal, since deleting j0 costs o(1); max p_j=o(1). The formal independent model avoidance probability P0(b)=∏(1−p_j) is ≥e^(-4W) for W large and all late intervals. It is also bounded above by e^(-cW); what matters below is its lower bound and use of the same P0 in all marked estimates.

Unweighted inclusion–exclusion through even Q and odd Q−1 brackets the actual probability of A_b. At comparison scale the Gaussian products give exactly the elementary symmetric polynomials in p_j. Their Qth term is ≤(3eW/Q)^Q, much smaller than e^(-4W). The total exact-atom errors from all orders ≤Q are ≤exp[−r_f+O(W log(WL))]+o(1), uniformly in b: each tuple's central lattice scale t^-q/2 cancels its O((L sqrt T)^q) site choices, with (2W+1)^q letter choices; all arithmetic additive errors beat any power of T. The model LLT o(1) relative shape, after summing the sites with fixed W,L,Q, tends to zero. Thus Pr_b(A_b)=P0(b)+O(e^-10W)+o(1); in particular ≍P0(b) uniformly. Model P0(b) converges uniformly to the same P0 because t=T+O(1).

The marked form fixes j∈I_b with W<|s|≤B*, i.e. B_j=s, and a nonnegative force weight Z_j described in §3. In the Bonferroni sum for A_b remove j (its exact atom already avoids the strip). The force-weighted local comparison of up to Q+1 sites gives Gaussian product gamma_t(j+1−s) times the mean comparator weight z_j, multiplied by the elementary symmetric polynomials on the *other* sites. Bounded forced primes change the means/variances only by O(1) and therefore this same product works. The Bonferroni upper is ≤2P0(b) gamma_t(j+1−s) z_j up to atom-comparison errors. Indeed the omitting-j avoidance probability is P0(b)/(1−p_j)=(1+o(1))P0(b), and its Qth error e^-100W multiplies this marked lattice factor rather than standing alone. Summed over j and s with the reciprocal-square weights below, arithmetic relative errors are at most exp[−r_f+O(W log(WL))] times the corresponding z_j assembly, and Gaussian o(1) errors vanish uniformly after summation at fixed parameters. Summed exponential absolute errors likewise vanish even after multiplying by all lattice choices and polynomial gcd weights. This pays the conditioning rather than using a rare-event subtraction by an unconditional tail.

## 3. A positive force majorant for the geometric angular tail

On a fixed incidence write g_j=gcd(b,j−j0), G_j=Ω(g_j), and let R_j be the repeated multiplicities of the stripped form. Away from rare huge powers paid by the size-biased note, let Q_j count distinct eligible primes above X^c through X^η for fixed small η, and R'_j count the truncated repeated powers below X^η. Primes above X^η have bounded total multiplicity by size and medium/high-repeat exceptions have exponentially small probability even at square-moment scale. For the true valuation V_j, the direct distinct-baseline comparison gives |V_j−U_j|≤Q_j+O_a(1)+G_j+R'_j. Also log₂ odd(d(n+j))≤2(R'_j+G_j+O_a(1)). Hence for B_j=s with |s|>W and fixed h=1,

    |e(d(n+j)/2^(j+1))−1| ≪ 2^(-|s|/2) + Z_j/s²,

where Z_j=C_a[(1+G_j)^2+Q_j²+(R'_j)²] is nonnegative. To verify the inequality, if |V_j−U_j| plus log₂ odd(d(n+j)) is <|s|/2, then s<0 gives a neutral term and s>0 gives effective angle ≤2^-s/2; otherwise bound by 2 and charge the appropriate squared variable. The little exceptional powers are paid separately by their unconditioned union probability, o(1) even compared with fixed P0. Z_j is an admissible sum of at most two forced prime or power indicators, plus the pointwise gcd factor. Its independent-prime comparator mass satisfies

    z_j ≪_a (1+G_j)^2 + log²(W+2),

since c^-1 is polynomial in W, the upper distinct-prime mean is O_a(log(W+2)), and the clipped repeat second moment uses convergent Euler weights. The same bounds hold after conditioning on a finite force class when applying the bank comparison.

Averaging over genuine incidences,
    E_I Σ_(j≠j0) γ_t(j+1−s) z_j ≪_a log²(W+2)
uniformly in each fixed s (indeed uniformly in s). To see this, first fix b and expand (1+G_j)^2 by positive divisor coefficients c(d) on d|g_j. Sum over j≡j0(b) mod d using Gaussian integral/maximum ≤C/d+C/sqrt(T), then sum b with *unnormalized* incidence weight ≪1/(b log N), giving ≪θ/d for b multiples of d. Divide by λ≍θ. The convergent d^-2 sum and the O((loglog T)^2/sqrt T) remainder give the claimed constant for the gcd part. The log²W term uses the plain Gaussian sum. This calculation keeps the adaptive residue and normalization rather than treating the j0 distribution as independent.

Insert the marked estimates from §2 and sum W<|s|≤B*. The total expected short central error on A is ≤C_a P0 log²(W+2)/W + o(1) plus a smaller factor exp[−r_f+O(W log(WL))]·O_a(log²W), absorbed here. The pure exponential term is even smaller. All expectations are in the genuine incidence law; the same P0 is retained in the marked upper and the unweighted lower, up to a vanishing uniform error as N→∞.

## 4. Unconditional outer sectors, with explicit exponential aperture

For j outside |j−T|≤L sqrt(T), use the true-valuation integrated atom envelope from the size-biased short-tail theorem with its earlier fixed cutoff (independent of W). In its deficit sum first split k=D_j positive at L sqrt(T)/2. Above that threshold the pooled tail is O_a(1/(L sqrt T)); below it the test valuation v=j+1−k has |v−T|≥(L/2) sqrt T−1. In the displaced-envelope lattice sum on j≡j0 mod d, use its tail integral O(1/(Ld)) plus O(1/sqrt T), then average b by the same adaptive order. Summing k with weights 2^-k/2+(1+k²)^-1 proves unconditional outer-site angular expectation O_a(1/L)+o(1). This includes all earlier small shifts j=0 etc and all unselected markers at those sites.

For |B_j|>B* at central sites, use only the one-coordinate all-atom arithmetic bound with max comparator atom O(T^-1/2), weighted by the positive force majorant Z_j (and also use weight 1 for the purely exponential contribution). On the O(L sqrt T) central sites, the adaptive divisor-stride average gives E_I Σ_j z_j/sqrt(T)≪_a L log²(W+2): for d|g_j first sum j in the adaptive progression inside the central band, giving O(L/d+T^-1/2) after normalization, then sum b multiples of d with the same incidence weight. The sum over |s|>B* of s^-2 is O(1/B*), so this central outer error is O_a(L log²(W+2)/B*)+o(1). Exponentially small additive comparison errors sum over at most O(log N) values of B_j with the reciprocal-square weights already summable; in particular they are not multiplied by the full lattice support. Taking B*=ceil(exp(50W)) places this term exponentially below P0. Enlarging B* costs no factor in the marked lattice comparison because its s-weights remain summable, and fixed B* leaves every marked atom in the central Gaussian box eventually. The original positive real tail after J is o(1) in incidence mean by the existing θ-scaled theorem.

Thus the unconditional outer costs are o_W(P0) as W→∞ (after N→∞), since L=e^(20W), B*≈e^(50W) and P0≥e^(-4W). Taking W fixed sufficiently large makes the total complementary angular error on A have expectation <P0/8, whereas Pr_I(A)≥P0/2. Markov gives positive probability in I that A holds and the complete complementary product differs from 1 by <1 (telescoping the individual errors; indices with nonpositive true deficits are already neutral). The half marker omitted from this complementary product is exactly −1. Hence the actual exp(2πi·2^(n−1)E) is within chord distance <1 of −1, inside the open mixed-word arc. The number F_good of successful incidences has ambient mean λ times that positive incidence probability; Cauchy–Schwarz with E F_good²≤E F² gives an ordinary positive fraction of actual starts. Counting 01 minus 10 telescopes to an endpoint bit difference, so both individual words would have positive lower density, uniformly on these short windows.

This proof program targets only h=1 and the mixed two-word arc; it does not give four length-two probabilities, an asymptotic, or normality. All conditional estimates use positive marked majorants and bounded-order bank comparisons, not independence of completed divisor angles.

## 5. Fixed-word extension

For fixed binary word of value u and length k use a fixed anchor A0=2^(2u); let b=A0 m≤N^θ with m odd squarefree and put j0=ω(m)+k+1. At n=bp−j0 the chosen coefficient is exactly

    d(n+j0)/2^(j0+1) = (2u+1)/2^(k+1),

the midpoint of the target cylinder, since p>b and (m,A0)=1. The allowed cofactors have reciprocal mass asymptotic to a positive constant depending on A0 times θ log N; the same squarefree harmonic proof also removing prime 2 gives this. First marker incidence mass is therefore ≍_(w)θ. Erasing j0>CT still costs o(1) by the previous high-Ω estimate. For two distinct marker positions put m=g0 m', ñ=g0 ñ', and then the actual cofactor gcd is A0 g0. The nonzero difference D=ω(m')−ω(ñ') must be divisible by A0 g0. The Sieving parameter length is H g0/(A0 mñ) and the singular factor from D/(A0g0) is bounded by the one from D/g0. The previous harmonic model and collision calculation therefore give a bounded second moment, retaining dependence on the fixed A0. At each unmarked shift the gcd in §3 has Ω≤Ω(A0)+Ω(gcd(m,j−j0)), and a proof of the same incidence averaging follows by pulling the fixed constant out of the weight and averaging the squarefree m instead. All other distribution, sieve and force-test estimates concern general b≤N^θ and already apply. Take W large depending on the fixed cylinder width (and the new fixed A0) so the complementary chord is smaller than the distance from the midpoint to its two cylinder endpoints. Thus these estimates give positive ordinary lower density of **every fixed word** in the eligible windows, not simply 01 and 10. It neither predicts the frequencies nor shows normality, and the constants are not asserted uniform for growing k.

## Source and measure clarification

The short-BV input is exactly the translated-interval, maximum-length short Bombieri–Vinogradov estimate written as equation (2) in [Perelli–Pintz–Salerno, pp. 529–530](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASNSP_1984_4_11_4_529_0.pdf), with the [Huxley–Iwaniec](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300006069) range ν<3ϑ−7/4−ε for 7/12<ϑ≤29/48. Use ϑ strictly below the actual cofactor-uniform short interval exponent, subdividing longer prime intervals into lengths ≤X^ϑ and summing absolute errors. Shrink the permissible modulus power below ϑ−1/2 to remove prime powers in aggregate; all cutoff shares were chosen inside this reduced power. See [marked tail](marked-tail.md) for the full weighted source calculation. Normalized ordinary and log-prime measures on each p≈X interval have pointwise Radon–Nikodym ratio 1+O(1/log X); thus every nonnegative marked upper or lower estimate transfers uniformly, not merely estimates for bounded indicators. The final ordinary incidence law and second moment are the genuine unweighted ones.
