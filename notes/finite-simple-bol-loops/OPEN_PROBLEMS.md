# Open problem board

This board organizes work; it does not certify that every intermediate assertion
in the project has been proved. Before using a proposed reduction, identify the
exact public statement and its review status. Stable local labels below are for
collaboration, not claims of new problems in the literature.

| ID | Question | A useful contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BOL-EXHAUST | What structural reduction makes the unrestricted proper-Bol branches exhaustive and controllable? | A precise reduction with proof, or a construction refuting a proposed exhaustion. |
| BOL-ODD-LIFT | How can admissible odd canonical-envelope lifts be classified without losing stabilizer and inversion data? | A lifting theorem with necessity, sufficiency, simplicity, and isomorphism conditions. |
| BOL-ODD-DEPTH | Which organizing invariants remain useful when nilpotency class, derived length, or Fitting height cannot be bounded uniformly? | A recursive structural theorem that accommodates unbounded depth; a proof that its proposed recursion covers every case. |
| BOL-ORBIT | How much can bimap/action/cocycle parameter orbits be simplified? | Canonical invariants, an exact equivalence algorithm for a stated slice, or a faithful embedding demonstrating unavoidable complexity. |
| BOL-ISOTOPE | What are the isotope classes and isomorphism classes within the new constructions? | An exact isotope formula and complete orbit computation, distinguishing changes of presentation from genuinely different loops. |
| BOL-BRUCK | Which radical/quotient/section data survive in simple Bruck loops and their isotopes? | Structural restrictions or constructive families beyond a bounded census; retain higher exponents and non-elementary radicals where relevant. |
| BOL-AFFINE | Which regular affine subgroups outside the classified root-type slices give distinct simple proper loops? | A new projection type, or a complete normal form with overlap and isomorphism analysis. |
| BOL-EVEN | How can the even non-Bruck route be reduced without discarding admissible radical extensions? | A compatible section construction or a uniform obstruction, not only a numerical group bound. |
| BOL-ACTIONS | Can a finite simple proper Bol loop have primitive one-sided multiplication group, and which full multiplication actions occur? | A proof or counterexample for a precise action class; explicitly say which multiplication group is used. |
| BOL-PRIOR-ART | Which constructions or reductions already occur in the literature? | Exact theorem locators, convention translations, and a comparison of hypotheses and conclusions. |

## Choosing work

Prefer a small, decisive statement over a broad unreviewable bundle. A negative
result is valuable when it kills a plausible reduction or shows why a parameter
cannot be bounded. Finite computation is most useful when it tests a named
structural statement or supplies a reproducible counterexample.

The priority is classification and structural understanding. Efficient generation
up to isomorphism is deferred. Unrelated speculative extensions are outside this
workspace's current scope.
