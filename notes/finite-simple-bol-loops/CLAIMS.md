## Normal layers exchanged by marked inversion

- [0136: Uniform fibers on exchanged normal layers](manuscripts/0136-exchanged-normal-layers.md). A proper paired layer forces at least two projected translations per stabilizer coset. For even fiber size, the projected stabilizer shares a prime with that size, at either quotient parity.

## Burnside and Hall–Sylow questions

- [0124: Infinite two-generator Bol loops at every odd prime exponent](bol-burnside/two-generators.md). The explicit loops are residually finite; at exponent three they show that the free right Bol loop on at least two generators is infinite.
- [0125: Matched translation groups at exponent three are locally finite](bol-burnside/matched-translations.md). Every finitely generated subgroup is a finite 3-group. Two individual matched translations generate at most 27 elements, sharply. [Review and finite checks for both Burnside results](bol-burnside/verification.md).
- [0114: Native Hall and Sylow failure in odd-order Bol loops](odd-bol-hall/README.md). A solvable loop of order 63 has no order-nine subloop. For each odd prime, full Sylow existence fails in an explicit family; a comparison isotope has subloops of the missing order. [Independent reviews and reproducibility](odd-bol-hall/REPRODUCE.md).
- [0126: An exact commuting-lift obstruction](odd-bol-hall/commuting-lifts.md). In the tilted Heisenberg family, each ordered independent quotient pair admits commuting native lifts precisely on one tilt hyperplane; at each nonzero tilt at least a fraction 1/*r* in every nonzero determinant class admits none. The proof and review distinguish native multiplication from commutation of permutations.

## Nonabelian radicals over the projective quotient

- [0122: Fixed-order multiplicity](projective-radicals/proofs/multiplicity.md). For every $`s\ge21`$, at least $`3^{s^3/4}`$ pairwise nonisotopic simple proper exponent-six loops have order $`20\cdot3^{119+38s}`$. Their faithful left groups have quotient PSL₂(19) and radicals of the same derived rank, $`19s`$.
- [0123: An optimal universal group host inside one radical](projective-radicals/proofs/universal-group-host.md). Every group of class at most two and exponent dividing three, of order at most $`3^k`$, embeds in a single native left-group radical; its logarithmic size is of order $`k^2`$, optimal even among arbitrary group hosts. This is not a native-subloop assertion. [Separate reviews and four programs](projective-radicals/verification.md).

## Simple loops of odd two-prime order

Setting $`f=\mathrm{ord}_q(p)`$, the [two-prime workspace](odd-prime-pair/README.md) sharpens the earlier threshold. [Verification and provenance](odd-prime-pair/VERIFICATION.md).

- [0127: Least exponent and complete first-degree census](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/all-core-prime-pair-minimum.md). Some order occurs exactly when $`p`$ divides $`q-1`$. The least exponent is $`1+\mathrm{lcm}(p,f)`$, with an [explicit census](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/two-weight-exhaustion-at-the-first-odd-prime-pair-degree.md) at equality.
- [0128: Gap immediately after the minimum](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/second-odd-prime-pair-degree-gap.md). For every admissible pair the next exponent is impossible; [the case $`p\mid f`$](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/second-degree-gap-with-p-dividing-f.md) completes the exclusion.
- [0129: Elementary-core ternary order-nine census](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/ternary-top-nine-elementary-core-classification.md). A [construction](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/third-degree-ternary-order-nine-top-construction.md) and exact count at order $`3^{f+3}q`$ with elementary abelian core and order-nine rotation. For $`q=19`$ this gives 15 types in this class.
- [0130: Limits on higher cyclic rotation groups](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/elementary-higher-top-mixed-weight-lower-bound.md). The earlier both-weight necessity for elementary cores is superseded by [0135](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md), which excludes those elementary cores outright. A [separate bound](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/no-saturated-higher-odd-prime-pair-top.md) for $`p\mid f`$ does not require an elementary core.

- [0131: Frattini quotient crowding](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/0131-frattini-quotient-crowding.md). Projecting the marked folder gives an exact uniform multiplicity, not necessarily a quotient loop; a fixed-central single axial packet per side is excluded.
- [0132: Multiplicity-one axial packets with fixed Frattini subgroup](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/0132-fixed-frattini-multiplicity-one.md). Even Frobenius degree rules out the stated nonabelian purely axial branch. For odd degree, the reciprocal-packet constraint applies.
- [0133: Repeated axial packets and physical central phases](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/0133-fixed-frattini-repeated-packets.md). A rounded isotropy bound holds without top semisimplicity. Exact phase formulas constrain collisions at zero and nonzero top; the all-even residual remains open.
- [0134: Exact two-type census at the prime-top family orders](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/0134-prime-top-two-types.md). At $`qp^{2p+2}`$ with $`\mathrm{ord}_q(p)=p`$, there are exactly two ordinary isomorphism types of finite simple proper left Bol loops. The order 85,293 case is included.

- [0135: Sharp minimum orders for abelian cores with higher cyclic rotation](odd-prime-pair/manuscripts/0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md). In the reciprocal two-axis model with cyclic top of order $`p^k`$ for $`k\ge2`$, the least abelian-core exponent is $`kd+k`$ with $`d=\mathrm{lcm}(p^k,\mathrm{ord}_q(p))`$, except for ternary order nine, where it is $`d+3`$. Elementary cores are excluded outside that exception.

## Odd-order obstructions and prime cubes

- [0115: Odd-order bounds and the least odd simple example](odd-degree/README.md). Odd cube-free orders have a normal series in the original loop with abelian factors of order prime or prime-square. For two-prime orders *p*ᵃ*q*, nonassociative simplicity requires *q* > *p* and *a* ≥ *p* + 1; at equality existence is characterized by the order of *p* modulo *q*. Two additional exclusions give the sharp odd minimum 1053, attained by Nagy’s earlier construction. No uniqueness claim.
- [0116: All Bol loops of prime-cube order](odd-degree/manuscripts/all-bol-loops-of-prime-cube-order.md). A classification up to isomorphism and isotopy for every prime *p* ≥ 5, including the centerless branches. A [general normal form](odd-degree/manuscripts/abelian-bruck-fibers-as-alternating-algebras.md) handles every finite odd Bol loop with abelian associated Bruck loop. [Separate proof checks](odd-degree/verification.md).

## Reading the IDs

A four-digit ID identifies a **mathematical claim or closely connected claim family**, not a manuscript version, author or level of confidence. Several component proofs can therefore carry the same prefix. An ID is never evidence that a claim is proved. Exact hypotheses, conclusions and boundaries are in the linked statement or manuscript.

- **0001–0017** preserve the existing claim ledger, including its broad compound claims. **0010 is the unresolved classification frontier**, not the existence theorem.
- **0018–0078** register distinct constructions, classifications and structural reductions developed beyond that ledger. This is a public indexing allocation, not a claim that the private ledger assigned these numbers.
- Numbered expositions contain the actual mathematics, not just catalogue wrappers. Supporting source drafts are linked until their consolidated exposition is available; date-stamped source folders do not define different claim IDs.
- No entry here asserts human referee acceptance, formal verification or literature priority. The original ledger's `proved` label is the project's claim of a proof, not an independent certification. `reviewed` records a check of a source revision, not automatic acceptance of later changes. For later manuscripts, checks are scope-specific; the default remains **research proof draft**.

## Scope and maintenance

This catalogue covers all seventeen original claim records and every manuscript currently released in this public workspace. It does not claim that unpublished source work has been exhaustively audited. A compound manuscript can contain lemmas, examples and conjectures beyond its headline family: its hypotheses and section labels control their status. A material new theorem should get a new ID rather than being hidden under0010; an alternative proof of the same result retains its ID. Corrections retain IDs and state which conclusion changed.

The crosswalk is available in [claim-manuscript-map.json](claim-manuscript-map.json). The next unallocated public claim number is 0137.
