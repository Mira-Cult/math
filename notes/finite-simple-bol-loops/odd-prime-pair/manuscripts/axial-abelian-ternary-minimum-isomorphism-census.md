# Isomorphism census at the ternary order-nine axial abelian minimum

**Mira Cult**

**Author proof pending independent review.** This extends the [physical ternary census](ternary-top-nine-elementary-core-classification.md) to every admissible prime pair within the purely axial abelian model, without assuming nine divides the Frobenius degree.

**Theorem.** Let $`q`$ be an odd prime with $`9\mid q-1`$. Put $`f=\mathrm{ord}_q(3)`$, $`d=\mathrm{lcm}(9,f)`$ and $`m=d/9=3^a r`$ with $`3\nmid r`$. Among simple proper left Bol loops of order $`q3^{d+3}`$ with canonical top of order nine and purely axial abelian canonical $`3`$-core, the number of isomorphism types is
```math
\frac{1}{3m}\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}3^{\,2\cdot 3^a\gcd(r,j)}.
```
The order is [the minimum within these hypotheses](0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md#4-sharp-order-and-the-ternary-exception). No assertion is made about mixed weights or nonabelian cores. For example $`q=73`$ has $`f=12`$, $`d=36`$, $`m=4`$ and gives 555 isotypes.

## 1. The equality group

At equality the moving axes are elementary abelian of dimension $`d`$ and $`C=C_N(B)`$ has positive and negative parts of dimension one. Indeed moving exponent at least nine would imply $`a\ge2d+2>d+3`$. In exponent three the sharp-order proof gives $`\dim A_1\ge d`$ and the mark count is $`a=\dim A_1+2+\dim C^-`$; the case $`C=0`$ is impossible with elementary moving part by its norm obstruction. Injection and surjection of the skew map $`U-U^{-1}`$ between the two signs, proved in the [ternary minimum argument](0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md#4-sharp-order-and-the-ternary-exception), now force equal dimension one and a nontrivial operator on $`C`$. By the graph surjection $`C`$ is elementary abelian too. With basis $`v,w`$ of signs $`-,+`$ the action is $`U=1+J`$, $`Jv=w,Jw=0`$ (the nilpotent operator in dimension two has one-dimensional positive kernel, and rescaling $`w`$ gives the relation). As previously the signed top lift $`h`$ has order nine.

The moving axis has exactly one multiplicity-one weight orbit under the top and Frobenius. Normalize the $`B_1`$ generator and its sign-mate so that this orbit is $`\langle 3,\alpha\rangle\le\mathbb F_q^\times`$, where $`\alpha`$ is the fixed multiplier of $`h`$ on $`B_1`$. This is cyclic of order $`d`$. Choose a generator $`\beta`$ of that group with $`\beta^{d/9}=\alpha`$: a generator's exponent can be chosen to have the prescribed unit class modulo nine and to avoid all other prime factors of $`d`$. Set $`b_0=d/f`$ and $`E=\mathbb F_{3^f}`$. On $`b_0`$ copies of $`E`$, let the generator of $`B_1`$ act on block $`i`$ by $`\zeta^{\beta^{-i}}`$, for a fixed primitive $`q`$th root $`\zeta`$. Let $`\sigma`$ shift blocks upward and wrap by the Frobenius $`\psi`$ satisfying $`\psi(\zeta)=\zeta^{\beta^{b_0}}`$. Here $`\beta^{b_0}`$ generates $`\langle3\rangle`$, so $`\psi`$ has order $`f`$. The block-wrap check gives $`\sigma b_1\sigma^{-1}=b_1^\beta`$. A normal basis of $`E`$ for $`\psi`$, started in block zero, gives a single length-$`d`$ permutation cycle for $`\sigma`$. Thus the chosen model has $`R=\sigma^m`$ of order nine, and implements the required multiplier $`\alpha`$.

Every actual signed module is isomorphic to this chosen model: permute blocks along its transitive order-nine top; identify the fields via the matched roots. The actual action between blocks is field-semilinear up to a scalar, with prescribed intertwiners on the fields, since these are generated by the root actions. Relative to the chosen $`R`$, eliminate all the scalars but one by rescaling along the block cycle, then remove the remaining norm-one wrap scalar by [the same Hilbert 90 argument](axial-abelian-minimum-isomorphism-census.md#1-the-signed-group-is-unique-after-normalization). Use the sign for the second moving axis. The fixed-ring degree of the wrap for $`R`$ is $`f/\gcd(9,f)=m`$; its units are denoted $`E_0^\times`$.

## 2. The same physical charts and affine orbits

The stabilizer is the graph over a rotation-stable hyperplane $`K`$ of the first axis and a true lift $`(x,c h)`$ after sign-fixed normalization of $`B_2`$ and removal of its first $`B`$ shift. In particular $`K`$ contains $`(R-1)A_1`$. The exact phase proof in the [earlier census](ternary-top-nine-elementary-core-classification.md#2-sufficiency-for-all-graph-maps-and-all-lift-phases) applies here: the chart is valid exactly when $`\eta((R-1)^2x)\ne0`$. The avoidance uses only $`R`$, $`U`$ and the actual axis coordinates. The transfer of core-freeness and all strict cuts from the irreducible-$`B`$ proof is justified as in the [ternary minimum construction](higher-top-augmentation-construction.md#the-ternary-order-nine-minimum): for the induced moving module the blocks are distinct simple rational $`B_i`$-modules, and the rotation permutes them transitively, so an actual normal subgroup contains zero or its entire moving axis. Sign exchanges axes.

For isomorphism, the unique Sylow $`q`$-subgroup of the true normalized stabilizer is $`B_2`$; hence its sign-mate $`B_1`$ and $`B`$ are preserved. A simultaneous scalar change on the generators of $`B_1,B_2`$ must preserve the weight orbit, so is $`\beta^i`$. The moving intertwiner for it is $`\sigma^i`$ followed by an element that centralizes both $`B_1`$ and $`R`$. Such a centralizer rescales each distinct rational block by a unit in $`E`$, with each multiplier transported by the prescribed field intertwiner along the block shifts. Its first-block multiplier must be fixed by the wrap of $`R`$, and conversely this is the only condition. Thus the scalar subgroup is $`E_0^\times`$. The same moving map is used on the second axis by the sign. The normalizer's possible images of $`h`$ have top power one (its multiplier on $`B_1`$ is fixed), first $`B_1`$ component zero (the projected stabilizer forces this), hence second $`B_2`$ component zero by invertedness. The remaining options are exactly the old inverted $`C`$-tilts $`(a'v+(a'/2)w)h`$; on $`C`$ the intertwiner scales both $`v,w`$ by one common scalar. These changes all preserve the signed relations.

For clarity, $`A_1`$ is free rank one over $`E_0[\langle R\rangle]`$: insert an $`E/E_0`$ normal generator in the first block and apply all nine powers of $`R`$. The quotient by $`R-1`$ is therefore an $`E_0`$-line. The augmentation in the single $`\sigma`$-cycle is a nonzero prime-field functional on this quotient, since any single basis vector has augmentation one; use the trace pairing of $`E_0`$ to normalize $`K`$ to its kernel by the scalar subgroup. Normalize lift augmentation to one by a scalar in $`\mathbb F_3^\times`$, move the lift $`x`$ by $`K`$ to one fixed first basis vector $`x_0`$, scale $`\eta((R-1)^2x_0)`$ to $`w`$, and tilt $`h`$ to make the final $`C`$ phase equal to $`u w`$ with $`u\in\mathbb F_3`$. The only remaining moving changes are $`\sigma^i`$: they preserve augmentation, and any residual scalar preserving its kernel is a prime-field scalar which must be one to preserve lift augmentation. Choosing the chart vector $`x_0`$ again gives exactly the [earlier affine action](ternary-top-nine-elementary-core-classification.md#3-the-exact-signed-equivalence-and-normalized-count) on $`(\eta,u)`$, with $`\sigma`$ now this length-$`d`$ permutation.

The [Burnside calculation](ternary-top-nine-elementary-core-classification.md#4-closed-evaluation-by-burnside) uses only that $`\sigma`$ is a single cycle of length $`9m`$ on the prime-field basis, that $`R=\sigma^m`$, and the same affine drift. Those now hold with $`9m=d`$ even when $`9\nmid f`$. Thus the displayed formula applies without change.
