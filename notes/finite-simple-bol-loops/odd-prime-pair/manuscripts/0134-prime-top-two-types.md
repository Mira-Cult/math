# Exactly two simple proper left Bol loops at the prime-top family orders

**Mira Cult**

**Theorem.** For every odd prime $`p`$ and every prime $`q`$ with $`\mathrm{ord}_q(p)=p`$, there are exactly two finite simple proper left Bol loops of order $`qp^{2p+2}`$, up to ordinary loop isomorphism. Proper means non-Moufang. The two types are distinguished by the square class of the true lower trace of the physical stabilizer's top generator. There is no extra hypothesis on the multiplication group or stabilizer.

The proof uses the [canonical reduction](prime-top-outer-reduction.md), [Hall-fixed exclusion](prime-top-hall-fixed-exclusion.md), [first-moving-layer classification](prime-top-moving-layer.md) and [explicit construction](prime-top-construction.md). [Verification and attribution](../PRIME_TOP_VERIFICATION.md) distinguish the uniform proof from finite replay.

## Proof of the classification
### Actual row and elimination of internal axial brackets

By the layer cover the cyclic top is p, the deficit is e=p, and the Frattini subgroup F is elementary central in N, with one moving lower degree-p axial packet on either side and no fixed sector. The first layer V=N/F has one moving degree-p axial packet per side and V0 with one negative dimension, hence a negative singleton or a length-two chain (−,+). Put C=C_N(B). It maps isomorphically to V0 by coprime lifting, so C is elementary. As in the [arbitrary fixed-sector result](prime-top-moving-layer.md#odd-two-prime-axial-first-movement-actual-fixed-sector), let P_i be the preimage of V_i in C_N(B_other), with lower F_i. These actual subgroups commute with each other, are D-normal, and [C,P_i]≤F_i by the Hall weights. Together with C they give unique coordinates on N. The physical W=H∩N maps onto C by the normally generating quotient D/(P_1P_2 B) and the top-zero coprime powering argument. If the crossed bracket from the fixed upper sector with the moving axes vanishes, the preceding fixed-sector proof already excludes the model: either the graph over K has index p and violates the proper FC signed cut or K is the whole first axis and its Frattini lies in H. So the crossed bracket is nonzero and the first and lower Hall fields on each axis align.

Temporarily align actual B2 in H by coprime conjugacy for necessary row analysis. Its moving layer in W∩F would be all of F2 if nonzero (B2 irreducibility), contradicting core-freeness. Also W has no second first axial projection. Otherwise [W,B2] inside W∩P2 surjects onto the entire irreducible V2. If the internal bracket on P2 or the p-power map V2→F2 is nonzero, the brackets or powers of these elements put all F2 in W. If both vanish, bracket with an actual W element projecting to a C value with nonzero second crossed action puts all F2 in W instead. The latter calculation holds because its second-axis component has zero internal bracket and the first axis commutes with P2. In every case this is impossible. Thus W lies in C P1.

Write $`S=W\cap F_1`$, $`s=\dim S`$, $`U_1=U\cap V_1`$ for the first kernel of the upper projection of $`U=WF/F`$, and $`d_1=\dim U_1`$. Strictness on actual F gives s≤p−1. The physical index and upper surjection give $`s+d_1=2p-2`$, since $`\dim C^-=1`$. If d1=p the actual bracket of the full V1 row cannot be nonzero, as its image would span irreducible F1 and force F1⊂W. Otherwise d1=s=p−1. Both U1 and S are genuine top-invariant hyperplanes. On V1 and F1 the top acts semilinearly over the degree-p Hall field K, with scalar factor removable by finite-field Hilbert 90 (actual top pth power is inner and acts trivially on these layers). Thus each is a regular Frobenius module; the hyperplane is the unique trace-zero hyperplane on each.

We claim in this latter case that the internal bracket $`\beta:\bigwedge^2 V_1\to F_1`$ is zero as well. If nonzero it spans the whole irreducible F1, so its contraction omega=Tr∘beta is nonzero, top invariant, and vanishes on the wedge of the trace-zero hyperplane U1 by actual W closure. Hence $`\omega=\mathrm{Tr}\wedge\lambda`$ for a linear functional $`\lambda`$, with $`\lambda R-\lambda=d\,\mathrm{Tr}`$ by top invariance; d≠0 since otherwise lambda would be a multiple of Tr in the regular module. Extend scalars to a splitting field. Choose a Hall weight basis e_i cycled by R on the p weights (R^p=1 on this layer). Then Tr(e_i) is the same nonzero value for all i: it is invariant under the cycle and nonzero. The relation on lambda gives omega(e_i,e_j) a nonzero common multiple of j−i. It is therefore nonzero for every distinct pair of Hall weight lines.

But the Hall weights of V1 and F1 are the same scalar multiple of the multiplicative orbit O=⟨p mod q⟩. There are two distinct orbit values whose sum is outside it. Indeed otherwise 1+y∈O for every y∈O distinct from 1; starting with any such y and successively adding 1 reaches 0 before 1 in the prime cyclic field F_q, impossible under the asserted closure and nonzero elements of O. Hall equivariance forces beta zero on that pair, contradicting the calculated nonzero entry of omega. This proves the claim. Sign likewise kills the internal second-axis bracket.

Now apply the actual trace-operator theorem of the preceding fixed-sector result, since the axes have no internal bracket. If codim S≥2 it gives d1=0, inconsistent with s+d1=2p−2. Thus s=d1=p−1, and the nonzero self-adjoint crossed operator vanishing on U1 has rank one. In regular Frobenius coordinates its image is the constants and its kernel the trace-zero hyperplane, so it is a nonzero scalar trace operator. The upper-to-axis-quotient map is onto by the actual FC cut of that result. In particular W projects onto all V1. The class-two p-power map would, if nonzero on V1, hit the full irreducible F1 and put it in W, impossible. It also vanishes on V2 by sign, and on fixed C by Hall equivariance. Hence N has exponent p, not p², with exactly the scalar crossed brackets of the rank-one construction.

### Signed Hall alignment, actual reference, and positive-terminal exclusion

For the actual equivalence proof align Hall via sign-fixed conjugation: a q-complement Q2 of H∩NB is conjugate to the initial B2 by an element of P2, since C and P1 centralize B2. Its sign image Q1 is conjugate to B1 by the paired element of P1. These conjugators commute, so their product is sign-fixed and simultaneously aligns the pair. Thus this operation is genuine signed-folder equivalence, not only conjugation preserving necessary cuts. After alignment W centralizes B2 and H∩NB=W×B2, with unique q-complement B2.

Choose an inverted h in N_D(B) over the normalized top. Its first projected Hall offset can be aligned to that of the actual projected H top by conjugating the reference h by a sign-fixed diagonal element of B: its first component is arbitrary and 1−R_B is invertible on B1, while the resulting second offset is absorbed by B2 inside H. This fixes B2 and preserves inversion of the reference; after that alignment choose the actual H top inside H and adjust by W to normalize its B2. One can choose the generator of the outer cyclic top to act by p on B1 (the order-p subgroup of F_q^× is unique), and choose B1's generator so its action on the first field is a fixed primitive qth root; sign fixes the reciprocal choices. Normalize semilinear scalar factors on V1,F1 by finite-field Hilbert 90; normalize the crossed scalar as c_head and the physical row as c_head=Tr(v) by field-coordinate scalings. More explicitly the bracket is initially b(c) v, its rank-one image is stable under the normalized Frobenius and consists of prime-field multiples of a single constant, and the row map to the trace quotient is nonzero; adjust the upper head and common field scale to make both stated identities. The positive terminal if present lies in the kernel of b. Then h^p is in C, is top-fixed and sign-negative. For any actual H top generator u h the V norm is a constant, of trace zero, and the C norm of u vanishes in the singleton and the length-two chain (sum an affine p-term progression in characteristic p). Its pth power lies in W, whose upper head is the trace of the first V vector. Thus h^p has zero upper head, hence is identity by sign negativity. The actual h therefore splits. The residual actual shear between V and F on each axis now has scalar trace zero (its norm is 0 since h^p=1) and can be removed by an axial section shear solving a Frobenius coboundary. Choose the reciprocal shear by sign. After this, the actual top on both axial grades is pure reciprocal Frobenius.

If C is length two, write its coordinates c0,c1 with signs (−,+) and top adding c0 to c1. The physical row has c0=Tr(v), arbitrary c1, and lower equation Tr(f)=lambda(v)+delta c1, with delta≠0 (otherwise the actual positive terminal, central in D, would be in H). Multiplying the true H top generator by a W element first kills its first V coordinate; then choose another row with v=0 and suitable c1 to set its first F trace equal to zero. There is no second-axis correction because the physical top has been adjusted inside H to normalize B2. The resulting g∈H of top one has V=0 and both lower traces zero. In the abelian subgroup F⊕C its sign image is conjugate to its inverse by some n there: the axial differences lie in the trace-zero images of the two reciprocal Frobenius differences, and the C difference is positive terminal and in the image of 1−R_C^−1. This is literal conjugacy; no bracket terms can arise for these n,g. As in the [individual construction](prime-top-construction.md#odd-two-prime-first-hall-moving-prime-top-rank-one-construction), it gives an actual outside inverter, whose odd power produces an outside involution and hence g in the mark difference set. This contradiction removes the length-two shape.

### Singleton parameters and the exact ordinary square classes

In the negative singleton C case the actual row is c=Tr(v) and Tr(f)=lambda(v). After removing its first V-coordinate by a W multiplier, let r be the actual H top generator's C-coordinate, and tau its first F trace. Its true closure gives $`\lambda(R-1)=r\,\mathrm{Tr}`$, exactly the scalar covariance of the construction. If tau=0, subtract its first F-coordinate using S=ker Tr in W; then c^r h itself is an inverted mark in H, forbidden. Conversely arbitrary r and nonzero tau pass the uniform physical construction: in the upper trace test at j≠0 the necessary trace is jr Tr(w1) while the physical row gives jr Tr(w1)+j tau. The norm, cuts, core and generation checks are unchanged. Thus these are exact allowable parameters.

There is a genuine signed ambient automorphism fixing B,C and sending h to c^d h (d additive upper coordinate), shearing both axes by (v,f)↦(v,f+k v) with k−R(k)=d. Solutions exist as constants have trace zero, and substitution checks the reciprocal axis actions and the relation h^p=1. It changes r by d and lambda by Tr(k·), whose derivative is d Tr. The remaining choice of k in F_p removes the trace-line ambiguity in lambda. Multiplication by S removes the choice of actual lower representative of tau. Thus r and lambda are not further isomorphism invariants.

An ordinary loop isomorphism induces an isomorphism of the canonical generated translation groups, preserves H and marks and hence also the unique sign. The actual unique B2 in H∩NB is preserved, so sign preserves B1 also. An image of h has outer form b h^j, with b∈B2 since a true H top has the same outer projection as h. Inversion under sign forces b=1, since B axes are exchanged. The prescribed scalar order-p action on the preserved B axis forces j=1. As the image normalizes actual B, it is therefore c^d h. On the first axial Hall field the maps on V,F are semilinear with the same Frobenius (the induced automorphism of B fixes the conjugacy relation of the primitive Hall scalar). Let their scalar factors be a,b; the whole BCH map may also have a lower shear linear in V. The map on C is a prime-field scalar s. Preservation of the row head c=Tr(v) on all V1 forces a=s by trace nondegeneracy, and bracket preservation forces $`b=sa=s^2`$. Hence on an actual $`V`$-zero top lift the trace transforms by $`\tau\mapsto s^2\tau`$: neither a V-dependent lower shear, the c-shift of h, nor Frobenius changes this computed factor. Conversely the simultaneous scaling v↦s v, c↦s c, f↦s² f on the two axes and C is signed and bracket-preserving, commutes with pure top, preserves the row head, and realizes the square adjustment (follow it by the r/lambda gauges already paid). Thus distinct square classes do not fuse and each is saturated by genuine physical equivalences. There are exactly two square classes in F_p^×, proving the uniform ordinary census.

## Order 85,293

Taking $`p=3`$ and $`q=13`$ gives exactly two types at order $`85{,}293`$. The [direct characteristic-three normal form](85293-normal-form.md), [Hall-moving exclusions](prime-top-moving-layer.md#odd-q13-order-85293-top3-e3-completeness) and [Hall-fixed exclusions](prime-top-hall-fixed-exclusion.md#odd-q13-order-85293-hall-fixed-cover) give an alternative proof at this order. This does not say that 85,293 is the least order of a finite simple proper left Bol loop.
