## Two physical normal forms at the scoped q=13, a=8 first-movement site
Consider order 13·3^8, top 3, first Hall-moving deficit e=3, one first axial packet per side with no mixed first weights, and the exchanged axial Frattini pattern with no Hall-fixed summand of F/Phi(F). This is the scope of the [fixed-upper axial theorem](prime-top-moving-layer.md#odd-two-prime-axial-first-movement-actual-fixed-sector) with the new minimum-budget specialization, not classification of all loops at this order or proof of their global minimum. In this exact site there are two unmarked loops: the [rank-one constructed model](prime-top-construction.md#odd-two-prime-first-hall-moving-prime-top-rank-one-construction) with true top lower traces +1 and −1. The proof below pays actual negative-head/top/hyperplane normalizations, not only projected equivalence.

### Forced first and lower shapes and actual reference

The lower group is elementary central in N by the cited theorem. Crossing is necessary and aligns the characters. The internal axial brackets vanish because the degree-three weights {1,3,9} have distinct pair sums in the negative orbit modulo 13 (the same holds after any scaling). Signed index gives dim C^−=8−1−6=1. The codimension-at-least-two lower row case would force dim C^−≥4; hence S=W∩F_1 is a hyperplane. The row budget then gives d_1=2; the rank-odd transfer with kernel containing U_1 has rank one, so the rank-three option cannot occur at a=8. Since f:C→V_1/U_1 is onto, W projects onto all V_1. If its actual cube map from V_1 to F_1 were nonzero, it would be an isomorphism of the irreducible Hall modules (the class-two cube map is additive because the commutator has exponent 3). Its image would put all F_1 in W, contradiction. The cube map on V_2 is killed by sign and on C by Hall fixedness, so N has exponent 3.

The Hall-fixed C is either a negative singleton or a length-two chain with a negative head and positive terminal: all signed Jordan heads there are negative by the [first-layer theorem](prime-top-moving-layer.md#odd-two-prime-frattini-covering-bound), and its negative dimension is one. Normalize the actual B as follows. A q-complement Q2 of H∩NB over outer B2 is conjugate to B2 by an element of P2, since C and P1 centralize B2. Applying sign gives its partner Q1 conjugate to B1 by the sign image in P1; the two conjugating elements commute. Their product is sign-fixed and aligns the entire Hall pair. This is a signed equivalence rather than merely conjugating H without its sign. After alignment W centralizes B2, so H∩NB=W×B2 and B2 is its unique q-complement.

Choose a true inverted h in N_D(B) covering the fixed outer top by the odd involution lifting argument. Its cube g0 lies in C, is top-fixed and sign-negative. On V_i and F_i the top is regular semilinear Frobenius, with scalar factors removable by finite-field Hilbert 90. The aligned crossed operator on V_1 is rank one, so the unique invariant lower hyperplane is S=ker Tr and after scalar normalization the row has head c0=Tr(v). More explicitly rank-one self-adjoint commuting T is a nonzero scalar trace operator, so rescale a common first V,F field coordinate and the negative upper head to make the crossed scalar b(c)=c0 and the row coefficient Tr(v). Sign fixes the normalization on the reciprocal axis. For any true H top generator u h, the cube in the first axial V coordinate is its Frobenius norm, a constant of trace zero. In C, the order-three norm of u is zero in either C shape, so the cube has C component g0. Since it lies in W and W's C head is the trace of its V, g0 has zero head. Its sign negativity now forces g0=0, so h really splits. On each axis the residual actual top shear between V and F is a scalar times Frobenius, with scalar trace zero as h³=1; change the B-equivariant BCH section by an axis scalar shear to kill it (solve a Frobenius coboundary). Choose the reciprocal shear by sign. Thus h genuinely acts by pure Frobenius on both axial grades.

### The positive terminal produces an actual collision

If C is the length-two chain, take coordinates c0,c1 with sign (−,+) and R_C(c0,c1)=(c0,c1+c0). The projected row has c0=Tr(v) and free c1, and its actual lower equation is Tr(f)=lambda(v)+delta c1. Here delta≠0 since otherwise the central actual positive terminal in C would lie in core-free H. Multiply a true H top generator u h by a suitable W entry to take its first V coordinate to zero (W surjects V_1) and remove its C-tail coordinate. Its remaining first F trace is an arbitrary scalar tau and its upper head some r. Now multiply it by a W entry with v=0 and chosen tail c1 to set the resulting lower trace to zero, using delta≠0. This produces g∈H of top one, with V=0 and both lower traces zero.

In the abelian subgroup F⊕C of N, conjugation by n on an element of top −1 adds (1−R_top^−1)n (with the appropriate reciprocal actions on axes). Thus t(g) is conjugate to g^−1 by an n in F⊕C: both lower coordinate differences are trace zero and therefore lie in the images of the respective Frobenius differences; the C difference is on the positive terminal and lies in the image of 1−R_C^−1. There is no hidden bracket term because these N-coordinates all lie in the abelian subgroup. This gives an actual outside inverter for g in D⋊⟨t⟩. An odd power of that inverter is an outside involution (D has odd order), still inverting g. Multiplying this involution by g gives another outside involution, so g is a product of two outside involutions, equivalently in Lambda^−1 Lambda. Since g is nonidentity in H, this contradicts actual Bol transversality. Thus the positive terminal shape is impossible.

### Singleton: exact gauges and the trace invariant

Now C is a single negative scalar, and the actual row has c=Tr(v), Tr(f)=lambda(v). Remove the top generator's first V-coordinate by multiplying by W; the resulting H lift u h has V=0, scalar r and lower F1 trace tau, with all second-axis coordinates zero (it normalizes B2). Actual top stability forces lambda(R−1)=r Tr. If tau=0, remove its lower vector by W∩F_1=S; then c^r h itself is an inverted mark of H, impossible. Conversely for either nonzero tau the [construction proof](prime-top-construction.md#odd-two-prime-first-hall-moving-prime-top-rank-one-construction) works with arbitrary r: its conjugation trace computation becomes forced Tr(f)=jr Tr(w1), but the actual offset is jr Tr(w1)+j tau, which never vanishes. All physical cuts are unchanged.

To normalize r, use the signed ambient automorphism sending h to c^d h (additive c-coordinate d), fixing C and B, and applying the same scalar shear (v,f)↦(v,f+k v) to both axes, where k−R(k)=d. This equation has a solution since the trace of a prime-field constant is zero. Direct substitution into the two reciprocal top actions gives compatibility: the inner shift on the second axis is the negative of that on the first. The actual row changes by lambda↦lambda+Tr(k·), its derivative changes by d Tr, and r changes by d. Thus normalize r=1. Adjusting k by a prime-field constant removes the remaining lambda ambiguity (the kernel of the derivative map on linear functionals is the trace line). The actual lower representative of fixed tau can be changed freely by S. This proves all data at a given tau are equivalent within the normalized signed families.

For nonfusion, an isomorphism of the loops induces an isomorphism of their canonical translation group and physical H, carrying sign to sign: marks generate the translation group, so the automorphism inverting each mark is unique. The characteristic p-core N, its Frattini F, and NB are intrinsic. Since B2 is the unique q-complement in H∩NB, the isomorphism preserves the normalized B2 and by sign also B1. It takes h into the normalizer of B. Its outer image is b h^j with b∈B2, because a physical u h lies in H and u∈N. Inversion under sign forces b=1 (the two Hall axes are exchanged), and comparison of the reciprocal scalar action on the preserved Hall axis forces j=1. Hence h maps to c^d h. On the first Hall axis the map on V and F is field-semilinear with the same Frobenius (the image of the Hall generator must match its irreducible scalar conjugacy); write the V factor a and the F factor b. If the map on C is scalar s∈F_3^×, preservation of c=Tr(v) on all of V forces a=s by nondegenerate trace, and bracket preservation forces b=s a=s²=1. Consequently the lower trace of a physical V-zero top generator is unchanged: neither the c-shift nor the V-dependent shear contributes to it, and Frobenius preserves trace. Thus tau=1 and tau=−1 cannot fuse. There are exactly two loops within the stated scope.
