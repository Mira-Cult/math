# Isomorphism census at the axial abelian higher-top minimum

**Mira Cult**

**Author proof pending independent review.** This concerns exactly the [sharp higher-top minimum](0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md), including the [ternary extension for rotation orders 27 and higher](0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md#4-sharp-order-and-the-ternary-exception). It does not cover [the ternary order-nine exception](axial-abelian-ternary-minimum-isomorphism-census.md), mixed weights, nonabelian cores, larger orders, or isotopy. The minimum-order theorem already rules out mixed weights at equality within the abelian case.

**Theorem.** Fix distinct odd primes $`p,q`$ and $`k\ge2`$ with $`n=p^k\mid q-1`$ and $`(p,k)\ne(3,2)`$. Write $`f=\mathrm{ord}_q(p)`$ and $`d=\mathrm{lcm}(n,f)`$. Among simple proper left Bol loops of order $`q p^{kd+k}`$ with canonical top of order $`n`$ and purely axial abelian canonical $`p`$-core, there are exactly $`p^{k-1}`$ isomorphism types. After choosing a fixed scalar $`\alpha`$ of order $`n`$ in $`\mathbb F_q^\times`$, their parameter is the action of the top lift of multiplier $`\alpha`$ on the cyclic stabilizer quotient of the first moving axis. It is an arbitrary $`u\in 1+p(\mathbb Z/p^k)`$.

## 1. The signed group is unique after normalization

Use the actual signed group $`D,H,T,t`$, with $`N`$ the abelian normal $`p`$-core, and choose the second $`q`$-axis $`B_2`$ to be the one recognized by the projected stabilizer. The equality theorem gives $`N=A_1\oplus A_2`$, with no $`B`$-fixed part, each $`A_i`$ homocyclic of exponent $`p^k`$ and rank $`d`$. The [actual graph argument](0135-higher-cyclic-abelian-cores.md#1-actual-lift-and-the-positive-projection) gives, after conjugacy of the point stabilizer to contain $`B_2`$ and diagonal $`B`$ normalization, the subgroup
```math
H=\langle (K,0),B_2,xh\rangle,\qquad [A_1:K]=n
```
where $`h`$ is inverted, has order $`n`$, acts on $`B_1`$ by the chosen multiplier $`\alpha`$ and on $`B_2`$ by $`\alpha^{-1}`$, and $`R`$ is the action of $`h`$ on the first axis. These normalizations can be performed by sign-fixed conjugations. If $`B_2`$ was not yet physically in $`H`$, there is a sign-fixed element of $`N`$ that does the normalization. Indeed any Sylow $`q`$-subgroup of $`H`$ mapping to the second quotient axis is conjugate to $`B_2`$ by some moving $`A_2`$ element, and the diagonal in $`A_1\oplus A_2`$ has the same effect as its $`A_2`$ coordinate because the first axis centralizes $`B_2`$. Sign-fixed conjugation preserves all of $`T`$ and gives an actual folder isomorphism. The additional diagonal $`B`$ normalization is likewise sign-fixed. After normalizing, $`H`$ has the displayed form by taking a true lift in $`N_H(B_2)`$ and removing its $`B_2`$ factor.

Let $`g=\gcd(n,f)`$, $`m=n/g`$ and $`E=\mathrm{GR}(p^k,f)`$. The moving mod-$`p`$ weight orbit under top multiplication and field Frobenius has size $`d`$ and exhausts the dimension of $`A_1/pA_1`$, so all its weight spaces have multiplicity one. By replacing the generator of $`B_1`$ and simultaneously its sign-mate generator of $`B_2`$, normalize this orbit to the one containing weight 1. The lifted separable factors of the $`q`$th cyclotomic polynomial over $`\mathbb Z/p^k`$ split $`A_1`$ into $`m`$ additive blocks; each is rank one over the associated copy of $`E`$ because its reduction modulo $`p`$ is rank one, and a lift of a generator spans by Nakayama with equal orders. Use a primitive $`q`$th root $`\zeta`$ to identify the blocks so that scalar weights are $`\zeta^{\alpha^{-i}}`$, $`0\le i<m`$.

The intertwining relation $`R b_1 R^{-1}=b_1^\alpha`$ makes $`R`$ the block shift upward (with an $`E`$-linear map before each wrap) and the wrap $`\lambda\varphi`$ for a unit $`\lambda\in E`$, where $`\varphi(\zeta)=\zeta^{\alpha^m}`$. In fact this follows by evaluating the intertwining on the given generator of the whole block coefficient ring. Rescale the block coordinates to make non-wrap transitions identity. Since $`R^n=1`$, the wrap satisfies $`\mathrm{Norm}_{E/E^\varphi}(\lambda)=1`$ (norm means the product of the $`g`$ conjugates). The twist can be removed by the same scalar rescaling on each block. For completeness, norm-one units are of the form $`\gamma/\varphi(\gamma)`$: solve first in the cyclic residue field multiplicative group and then inductively correct modulo $`p^r`$. A norm-one error $`1+p^r z`$ modulo $`p^{r+1}`$ has trace-zero residue $`z`$. Additive trace-zero equals the image of $`1-\varphi`$ by a relative normal basis, so multiplication by a quotient of $`1+p^r w`$ and its conjugate removes the error. This handles also $`g`$ divisible by $`p`$; $`g=1`$ is immediate. Choose the second axis coordinates by the action of $`t`$. All normalized signed groups are therefore the single [induced augmentation group](higher-top-augmentation-construction.md).

## 2. The stabilizer quotient is cyclic and the lift is primitive

Write $`Q_0=A_1/K`$ and again $`R`$ for its induced action. This finite abelian $`p`$-group has order $`p^k`$. If it were not cyclic, its exponent would divide $`p^{k-1}`$. For $`s=p^{k-1}`$ use the same norm expansion $`\Sigma_s(R)=\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}\binom{s}{i+1}(R-1)^i`$ as in the minimum proof. The coefficients have valuation $`k-1-r`$ for $`r=\nu_p(i+1)`$. The $`r=0`$ terms vanish by exponent; if $`r\ge1`$ then $`i\ge p^r-1\ge r+1`$, so the term has degree at least $`k`$ in the commuting nilpotents $`p,R-1`$. Every such product kills $`Q_0`$ (each nonzero intermediate image strictly shrinks). Hence $`(xh)^s`$ would have first component in $`K`$, yielding a forbidden pure inverted $`h^s`$ in $`H`$. Thus $`Q_0`$ is cyclic.

Put $`R_0=\mathbb Z/p^k`$. Choose a surjection $`F:A_1\to R_0`$ with kernel $`K`$. Then $`F(Ra)=u F(a)`$ for a unique $`p`$-power-order unit $`u`$, hence $`u\equiv1\bmod p`$. For an integer lift of $`u`$ congruent to 1 modulo $`p`$, the geometric sum $`\Sigma_j(u)`$ has $`p`$-valuation exactly $`\nu_p(j)`$ for every $`j>0`$: this follows from $`\nu_p(u^j-1)=\nu_p(u-1)+\nu_p(j)`$ at odd $`p`$ and by direct calculation if $`u=1`$. If $`F(x)`$ were not a unit then $`\Sigma_s(u)F(x)=0`$, again yielding pure inverted $`h^s`$ in $`H`$. Normalize $`F(x)=1`$ by a scalar change of the target. Conversely every such $`F,u,x`$ yields a genuine folder: $`u^n=1`$, $`\Sigma_n(u)=0\bmod n`$, so the lift closes. For nonzero projected top $`j`$, the [two moving negative graphs](higher-top-augmentation-construction.md) force a first coordinate in $`(R^j-1)A_1`$ and hence an $`F`$-value in $`(u^j-1)R_0`$. But an $`H`$ element there has $`F`$-value $`\Sigma_j(u)`$. Its valuation is strictly less than that of $`u^j-1`$ (or that ideal is zero), so collision is impossible. For zero top the previous collision proof is unchanged. Counts, generation, core-freeness and every strict cut are as in the induced construction: only $`F(Ra)=uF(a)`$ and $`F(p^r x)=p^r\ne0`$ for $`r<k`$ are needed instead of invariance of $`F`$.

## 3. Exactly one type per unit

Let $`E_0=E^\varphi`$. Choose a relative normal generator modulo $`p`$ and lift it to $`E`$; its $`g`$ conjugates form an $`E_0`$-basis by reduction modulo $`p`$. Inserting it in the first block gives $`z`$ whose $`n`$ translates form an $`E_0`$-basis of $`A_1`$ under the normalized $`R`$. In particular every equivariant $`F`$ is determined by the $`R_0`$-linear functional $`\ell(y)=F(yz)`$ on $`E_0`$: on $`R^i(yz)`$ it has value $`u^i \ell(y)`$. Conversely any such functional defines an equivariant $`F`$ because $`u^n=1`$. The trace pairing on $`E_0/R_0`$ is perfect by reduction to the separable field trace pairing, so $`\ell=\mathrm{Tr}(\lambda\,\cdot)`$ for a unique $`\lambda\in E_0`$. Surjectivity is equivalent to $`\lambda`$ a unit, again by reduction. Common multiplication by any unit of $`E_0`$ on the two axes commutes with $`B,h,t`$ and is transitive on these primitive functionals. For fixed normalized $`F`$, its allowed vectors $`x`$ differ by $`K`$ and give the very same subgroup. Thus at most one isotype per $`u`$, and each $`u`$ is realized.

Conversely an isomorphism induces the faithful group isomorphism taking the mark set and the true stabilizer to their counterparts. It intertwines the canonical inversion automorphism since that automorphism inverts each generating mark. It preserves $`N`$, and it sends the literal $`B_2`$ to $`B_2`$ since this is the unique Sylow $`q`$-subgroup of $`H`$ in the normalized chart (it is normal there). Thus it preserves $`B_1=t(B_2)`$ and $`B`$, and preserves the first moving axis. A normalized $`h`$ is sent to an element $`b h`$ in the $`B`$-normalizer: its multiplier on $`B_1`$ is fixed since automorphisms of the cyclic group commute, and $`N\cap N_D(B)=1`$. Since the image of the actual lift $`xh`$ has no first-axis $`B_1`$ coordinate modulo $`N`$, the $`B_1`$ component of $`b`$ is trivial. Since $`b h`$ is inverted by the sign, a remaining pure $`B_2`$ component is also trivial (the sign swaps axes and inversion preserves the axis of that component). Hence it sends $`h`$ to $`h`$ and induces an equivariant isomorphism of the two cyclic $`Q_0`$. The unit $`u`$ cannot change under such an isomorphism. This proves the census.
