### 1. Universal Imprimitivity and $G$-Loop Property of Exact-Factorization Left Bol Loops

#### Proposition 1.1 (Universal Imprimitivity of Exact-Factorization $\operatorname{LMlt}(Q)$)
Let $X$ be a group with an exact factorization $X = Y_0 Y_1$ ($Y_0 \cap Y_1 = \{1_X\}$) satisfying $|Y_0| \ge 2$ and $|Y_1| \ge 2$, and let $(\hat{G}, \hat{H}, S)$ be the canonical exact-factorization left Bol folder inside $X \times X$ (`C0002` Proposition 1.1(1)–(2)), with $S = \{(x, x^{-1}) : x \in X\}$, $\hat{G} = \langle S \rangle$, and $\hat{H} = \hat{G} \cap (Y_0 \times Y_1)$. Then $\hat{G}$ (and hence $\operatorname{LMlt}(Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}) \cong \hat{G} / \operatorname{Core}_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H})$) acts **imprimitively** on $\hat{G} / \hat{H} \cong Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$.

*Proof*: Put $M_0 := \hat{G} \cap (Y_0 \times X)$, so $\hat{H} \le M_0 \le \hat{G}$.
1. Pick $1_X \neq y_0 \in Y_0$ ($|Y_0| \ge 2$). Then $s_0 := (y_0, y_0^{-1}) \in S \subseteq \hat{G}$ lies in $M_0$; if $s_0 \in \hat{H} \le Y_0 \times Y_1$, then $y_0^{-1} \in Y_1$, whence $y_0 \in Y_0 \cap Y_1 = \{1_X\}$, contradiction. Thus $\hat{H} \lneq M_0$.
2. Pick $1_X \neq y_1 \in Y_1$ ($|Y_1| \ge 2$). Then $s_1 := (y_1, y_1^{-1}) \in S \subseteq \hat{G}$ satisfies $s_1 \notin Y_0 \times X$ (since $y_1 \notin Y_0$), so $M_0 \lneq \hat{G}$.

More precisely, every $g = s h_0 \in \hat{G}$ ($s = (x, x^{-1}) \in S$, $h_0 \in \hat{H} \le Y_0 \times Y_1$) lies in $M_0$ iff $x \in Y_0$, so $[M_0 : \hat{H}] = |Y_0| \ge 2$ and $[\hat{G} : M_0] = |Y_1| \ge 2$. Since $\operatorname{Core}_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H}) \le \hat{H} \lneq M_0 \lneq \hat{G}$, the image of $M_0$ defines a proper nontrivial $\operatorname{LMlt}$-block system of $|Y_1|$ blocks of size $|Y_0|$. In particular, for Nagy's $Q_{60} = Q_{(A_5, C_5, A_4)}$, $\hat{G} = A_5 \times A_5$ acts with point stabilizer $\hat{H} = C_5 \times A_4$ (not the diagonal subgroup $\Delta(A_5)$), so $\operatorname{LMlt}(Q_{60})$ acts imprimitively rather than of primitive `SD_2` or `HS` type. $\square$

#### Theorem 1.2 (Universal $G$-Loop Theorem for Exact-Factorization Left Bol Loops)
Every exact-factorization left Bol loop $Q = Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ (for any group $X = Y_0 Y_1$ with $Y_0 \cap Y_1 = \{1_X\}$, of arbitrary order and exponent) is a **$G$-loop**: every loop isotopic to $Q$ is isomorphic to $Q$.

*Proof*:
1. **Arbitrary-exponent principal-isotope folder reduction**: Let $(Q, *)$ be any left Bol loop with canonical faithful folder $(G, H, K) = (\operatorname{LMlt}(Q), \operatorname{LInn}(Q), \{L_x : x \in Q\})$. Every loop isotopic to $(Q, *)$ is isomorphic to a principal isotope $(Q, \odot_{a,b})$ ($a, b \in Q$) with product $x \odot_{a,b} y := R_a^{-1}(x) * L_b^{-1}(y)$. By `C0001` Theorem 1.5(1), $\Theta_a = (L_a R_a, L_a^{-1}, L_a) \in \operatorname{Atp}(Q)$, meaning $L_a(w * z) = (L_a R_a)(w) * L_a^{-1}(z)$ for all $w, z \in Q$. Setting $X := L_a(x)$, $Y := L_a(y)$, and $d := a * (b * a) \in Q$ (so $L_d = L_a L_b L_a$), we obtain
   $$L_a(x \odot_{a,b} y) = (L_a R_a)\bigl(R_a^{-1}(L_a^{-1}(X))\bigr) * L_a^{-1}\bigl(L_b^{-1}(L_a^{-1}(Y))\bigr) = X * (L_a L_b L_a)^{-1}(Y) = X * L_d^{-1}(Y) = L_a(x) \odot_{1_Q, d} L_a(y),$$
   so $L_a : (Q, \odot_{a,b}) \xrightarrow{\sim} (Q, \odot_{1_Q, d})$ is a loop isomorphism.
   In $(Q, \odot_{1_Q, d})$, the identity is $e' = d = L_d(1_Q)$ with stabilizer $G_{e'} = k_0 H k_0^{-1}$ ($k_0 := L_d \in K$, $k_0^{-1} = L_{d^{-1}} \in K$), and the left multiplications form $K k_0^{-1}$ (closed under $(x k_0^{-1})^{-1} = (k_0 x^{-1} k_0) k_0^{-1} \in K k_0^{-1}$ and $(x k_0^{-1})(y k_0^{-1})(x k_0^{-1}) = x(k_0^{-1} y k_0^{-1})x k_0^{-1} \in K k_0^{-1}$, with $\langle K k_0^{-1} \rangle = G$ since $1_G, k_0^{-1} \in K k_0^{-1}$). Thus $(Q, \odot_{1_Q, d})$ is a left Bol loop with faithful folder $(G, k_0 H k_0^{-1}, K k_0^{-1})$. Conjugating inside $G$ by $k_0^{-1}$ maps $(G, k_0 H k_0^{-1}, K k_0^{-1}) \xrightarrow{\sim} (G, H, k_0^{-1} K)$.
2. **Factor-conjugation in $X \times X$**: Now let $Q = Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ with $(\hat{G}, \hat{H}, S)$ inside $X \times X$ ($S = \{(x, x^{-1}) : x \in X\}$, $\hat{G} = \langle S \rangle$, $\hat{H} = \hat{G} \cap (Y_0 \times Y_1)$), and let $s_0 = (a, a^{-1}) \in S$ ($a \in X$) lift $k_0 \in K$. Because $X = Y_0 Y_1$, inversion gives $X = X^{-1} = Y_1^{-1} Y_0^{-1} = Y_1 Y_0$, so $a = y_1 y_0$ for unique $y_1 \in Y_1, y_0 \in Y_0$. Put $h_* := (y_0^{-1}, y_1) \in Y_0 \times Y_1$. As $z$ ranges over $X$, $x := y_1 z y_0$ ranges bijectively over $X$, and
   $$h_* (z, z^{-1}) h_*^{-1} = (y_0^{-1} z y_0,\; y_1 z^{-1} y_1^{-1}) = \bigl((y_1 y_0)^{-1}(y_1 z y_0),\; (y_1 y_0)(y_1 z y_0)^{-1}\bigr) = (a^{-1} x,\; a x^{-1}) = s_0^{-1}(x, x^{-1}).$$
   Hence $h_* S h_*^{-1} = s_0^{-1} S$. Since $1_{\hat{G}}, s_0^{-1} \in s_0^{-1} S$, we have $\langle s_0^{-1} S \rangle = \langle S \rangle = \hat{G}$, so conjugation by $h_* \in Y_0 \times Y_1$ normalizes $\hat{G}$, normalizes $\hat{H} = \hat{G} \cap (Y_0 \times Y_1)$ (and hence $\operatorname{Core}_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H})$), and maps $S$ onto $s_0^{-1} S$. Passing modulo $\operatorname{Core}_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H})$ gives a folder isomorphism $(G, H, K) \xrightarrow{\sim} (G, H, k_0^{-1} K)$, so $(Q, \odot_{a,b}) \cong (Q, *)$ by `C0001` Theorem 1.4(2). $\square$

**Corollary 1.3 (Burnside-Free Exact-Factorization Obstruction)**: *Any left Bol loop whose isotopy class contains at least two non-isomorphic loops (in particular, all $4$ simple loops of order $96$ in `C0006` and all $20$ simple loops of order $1536$ in Theorem 2.1 below) is not isomorphic to any exact-factorization loop $Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$.*

### 2. Complete $20$-Class Principal-Isotope Classification of $Q_{1536}$

#### Theorem 2.1 (The $20$ Simple Proper Left Bol Loops Isotopic to $Q_{1536}$)
Let $Q_{1536}$ be the deformed exponent-$2$ simple proper left Bol loop of order $1536$ (`C0005` Theorem 2.1, $k = 1$; `N07E` Proposition 5.1 and Theorem 5.2), with faithful folder $(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{K})$ where $\mathcal{G} = G \ltimes U$ ($|G| = 3840$, $|U| = 2^8 = 256$, $|\mathcal{G}| = 983{,}040$, $|\mathcal{H}| = 640$, $|\mathcal{K}| = 1536$).
1. **Centerlessness and $\operatorname{Aut}(Q_{1536})$**: Direct stabilizer check (`code/known_constructions.py`, `code/test_wave2_census.py`) certifies $N_{\mathcal{G}}(\mathcal{H}) = \mathcal{H}$ ($N_\rho(Q_{1536}) = \{1\}$). Because $Z(\mathcal{G}) \le N_{\mathcal{G}}(\mathcal{H}) = \mathcal{H}$ is normal in $\mathcal{G}$, faithfulness $\operatorname{Core}_{\mathcal{G}}(\mathcal{H}) = \{1\}$ immediately gives **$Z(\mathcal{G}) = \{1\}$** (also directly visible from $Z(G) = 1$ and $U^G = \{0\}$). Hence `C0006` Theorem 1.1 applies directly to $(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{K})$.
   Using Sims bottom-up point-stabilizer search (`sims_automorphism_group`) on a $6$-element generating base $B \subset \mathcal{K}$ (`\langle B \rangle = \mathcal{G}`), $|\operatorname{Aut}(Q_{1536})| = 1280 = 80 \cdot 16$.
