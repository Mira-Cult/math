# Proof of Claim `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0002`

We use the folder equivalence, Moufang primitivity obstruction, nuclei formulas, and exact normal-subloop criterion established in Claim `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0001`.

---

### 1. Exact-Factorization Folders, Nuclei, and Necessity of $X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X$

#### Proposition 1.1 (Exact-Factorization Left Bol Loops and Canonical Folder $\hat{G}$)
Let $X$ be a finite group with an exact factorization $X = Y_0 Y_1$ ($Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1$, $Y_0 \neq 1$, $Y_1 \neq 1$) that is core-free ($\operatorname{Core}_X(Y_0) = \operatorname{Core}_X(Y_1) = 1$).
1. **Folder structure and explicit product**: In $X \times X$, set $H_{\mathrm{amb}} := Y_0 \times Y_1$ and $S := \{(x, x^{-1}) : x \in X\}$. Clearly $(1, 1) \in S$, $S^{-1} = S$, and $(x, x^{-1})(y, y^{-1})(x, x^{-1}) = (x y x, x^{-1} y^{-1} x^{-1}) \in S$. The transversal property follows from the conjugated-intersection argument in the [intrinsic criterion manuscript](../manuscripts/exact-factorization-criterion.md#proof-necessity), necessity proof, first paragraph after the generated-folder discussion. Thus, by `C0001` Theorem 1.3, $(X \times X, Y_0 \times Y_1, S)$ is a left Bol folder. Identifying $X \leftrightarrow S$ via $x \leftrightarrow (x, x^{-1})$, for $x, y \in X$ write $(x y)^{-1} = a_0 a_1$ and $x^{-1} y^{-1} = b_0 b_1$ ($a_i, b_i \in Y_i$); then $z := a_1^{-1} b_0^{-1} = (b_0 a_1)^{-1} \in X$ satisfies $(x y)^{-1} z = a_0 a_1 a_1^{-1} b_0^{-1} = a_0 b_0^{-1} \in Y_0$ and $z^{-1} = b_0 a_1 \in b_0 Y_1 = x^{-1} y^{-1} Y_1$, so $(z, z^{-1}) \in S \cap (x, x^{-1})(y, y^{-1}) H_{\mathrm{amb}}$, proving **$x \circ y = a_1^{-1} b_0^{-1} = (b_0 a_1)^{-1}$**.
2. **Generated group $\hat{G} = \langle S \rangle$**: For all $x, y \in X$, $(x, x^{-1})(y, y^{-1}) = (x y, x^{-1} y^{-1})$ has product of coordinates $(x y)(x^{-1} y^{-1}) \in X'$. Conversely, for any $x, y \in X$, $(x, x^{-1})(y, y^{-1})(x y, (x y)^{-1})^{-1} = (1, [x^{-1}, y^{-1}]) \in \langle S \rangle$, so $1 \times X' \le \langle S \rangle$, and conjugating by $\sigma(u, v) = (v, u)$ (which preserves $S$) gives $X' \times 1 \le \langle S \rangle$. For any $(u, v) \in X \times X$ with $u v \in X'$, $(u, v) = (u, u^{-1})(1, u v) \in S(1 \times X') \subseteq \langle S \rangle$. Hence
   $$\hat{G} := \langle S \rangle = \{(u, v) \in X \times X : u v \in X'\} \quad (|\hat{G}| = |X| \cdot |X'|), \qquad \hat{H} := \hat{G} \cap (Y_0 \times Y_1) \quad (|\hat{H}| = |X'|).$$
3. **Necessity of $X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X$ for simplicity, faithfulness, properness, and nuclei**:
   - *Necessity*: Suppose $M := X' Y_0 \lneq X$ (or symmetrically $X' Y_1 \lneq X$). Because $X' \le M$, $M \trianglelefteq X$ with $X / M$ nontrivial abelian. For every $(u, v) \in \hat{H}$, $u \in Y_0 \le M$ and $u v \in X' \le M \implies v \in M$, so $\hat{H} \le N := (M \times M) \cap \hat{G} \trianglelefteq \hat{G}$ ($N \lneq \hat{G}$ since $|M| < |X|$). By `C0001` Theorem 2.2 (with $U = \hat{H} N = N$), $S \cap N = \{(x, x^{-1}) : x \in M\}$ is a **proper nontrivial normal subloop** $M \trianglelefteq Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ of index $[X : M] > 1$ (since $Y_0 \neq 1 \implies M \neq 1$).
   - *Faithfulness, non-Moufang property, and nuclei under $X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X$*: Assume $X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X$ (since $Y_i \neq 1$ and $\operatorname{Core}_X(Y_i) = 1$, $X$ is nonabelian, so $X' \neq 1$). For any $y_0 \in Y_0$, $X = X' Y_1$ gives $y_1 \in Y_1$ with $y_0 y_1 \in X'$, so $\operatorname{pr}_1(\hat{H}) = Y_0$ and symmetrically $\operatorname{pr}_2(\hat{H}) = Y_1$. If $N_0 := \operatorname{Core}_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H})$, then $X' \times X' \le \hat{G}$ and $\hat{H}$ both normalize $N_0$, so $X = X' Y_i$ normalizes $\operatorname{pr}_i(N_0) \le Y_i$, forcing $\operatorname{pr}_i(N_0) \le \operatorname{Core}_X(Y_i) = 1$. Thus **$(\hat{G}, \hat{H}, S)$ is already faithful** ($\operatorname{LMlt}(Q) \cong \hat{G}$). Since $|\hat{H}| = |X'| > 1$, $Q$ is nonassociative; moreover, $1 \neq X' \times 1 \trianglelefteq \hat{G}$ has base orbit $\{(x, x^{-1}) \in (X' \times 1)\hat{H}\} \subseteq \{(x, x^{-1}) : x \in Y_1\} \subsetneq S$ (as $|Y_0| > 1$), so $\operatorname{LMlt}(Q)$ is imprimitive on $Q$. By `C0001` Lemma 1.2, **no simple exact-factorization loop $Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ with $Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1$ is Moufang**. Finally, since $\operatorname{pr}_i(\hat{H}) = Y_i$ and $\hat{H} = (Y_0 \times Y_1) \cap \hat{G}$, $(x, x^{-1}) \in S \cap N_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H}) \iff x \in N_X(Y_0) \cap N_X(Y_1)$, proving **$N_\rho(Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}) = N_X(Y_0) \cap N_X(Y_1)$** (while $N_\lambda = N_\mu = \{1\}$ when simple nonassociative by `C0001` Theorem 1.5(1)).

### 2. Simplicity and Complete Isomorphism Theorem for Almost-Simple Exact Factorizations

#### Theorem 2.1 (Simplicity and Complete Isomorphism Criterion for `N07` Theorem 3.6)
Let $X$ be a finite almost-simple group with nonabelian simple socle $T = \operatorname{Soc}(X)$ ($T \trianglelefteq X \le \operatorname{Aut}(T)$), and let $X = Y_0 Y_1$ ($Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1$, $\operatorname{Core}_X(Y_0) = \operatorname{Core}_X(Y_1) = 1$, $Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1$) satisfy $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$.
1. **Simplicity and properness**: Since $T$ is nonabelian simple with $C_X(T) = 1$, $T \le X'$ and $\operatorname{Soc}(\hat{G}) = T \times T$, whose only nontrivial normal subgroups are $T \times 1$, $1 \times T$, and $T \times T$. Given any $(u, v) \in \hat{G}$ ($u v \in X'$), write $u = t_0 y_0$ ($t_0 \in T, y_0 \in Y_0$) and $v = t_1 y_1$ ($t_1 \in T, y_1 \in Y_1$) using $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$; then $u v = t_0 (y_0 t_1 y_0^{-1})(y_0 y_1) \in T(y_0 y_1) \implies y_0 y_1 \in T X' = X'$, so $(y_0, y_1) \in \hat{H}$ and $(u, v) = (t_0, t_1)(y_0, y_1) \in (T \times T)\hat{H}$, proving $(T \times T)\hat{H} = \hat{G}$. Now inspect any nontrivial normal subgroup $1 \neq N \trianglelefteq \hat{G}$: since $C_{\hat{G}}(T \times T) = 1$, $N \cap (T \times T) \in \{T \times 1, 1 \times T, T \times T\}$:
   - If $T \times T \le N$, then $\hat{H} N \ge (T \times T)\hat{H} = \hat{G}$, so $[\hat{G} : \hat{H} N] = 1$ ($U = \hat{G}$).
   - If $N \cap (T \times T) = T \times 1$, then $[N, 1 \times T] \le N \cap (1 \times T) = 1 \implies N \le C_{\hat{G}}(1 \times T) = (X \times 1) \cap \hat{G} = X' \times 1$. Then $(x, x^{-1})^{-1}(y, y^{-1}) \in N \implies y = x$ from the second coordinate, so $|S N / N| = |S| = |X|$; meanwhile $\operatorname{Core}_{\hat{G}}(\hat{H}) = 1$ implies $N \not\le \hat{H}$, so $|\hat{H} N| > |\hat{H}| = \frac{|\hat{G}|}{|X|}$ and $[\hat{G} : \hat{H} N] < |X| = |S N / N|$. Symmetrically if $N \cap (T \times T) = 1 \times T$.
   By `C0001` Theorem 2.2(3) and Proposition 1.1(3), **$Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ is a finite simple proper left Bol loop**! $\square$

2. **Complete isomorphism classification and $\operatorname{Aut}(Q)$**: Let $\mathcal{T} = (X, Y_0, Y_1)$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{T}} = (\widetilde{X}, \widetilde{Y}_0, \widetilde{Y}_1)$ be two core-free exact factorizations of almost-simple groups ($T \le X \le \operatorname{Aut}(T)$, $\widetilde{T} \le \widetilde{X} \le \operatorname{Aut}(\widetilde{T})$, $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$, $\widetilde{X} = \widetilde{T}\widetilde{Y}_0 = \widetilde{T}\widetilde{Y}_1$, $Y_i, \widetilde{Y}_i \neq 1$). We prove **$Q_{\mathcal{T}} \cong Q_{\widetilde{\mathcal{T}}}$ iff there exists a group isomorphism $\alpha : X \xrightarrow{\sim} \widetilde{X}$ with $\{\alpha(Y_0), \alpha(Y_1)\} = \{\widetilde{Y}_0, \widetilde{Y}_1\}$**.
   - *($\Leftarrow$)*: An isomorphism $\alpha : X \xrightarrow{\sim} \widetilde{X}$ with $\alpha(Y_i) = \widetilde{Y}_i$ induces the folder isomorphism $\alpha \times \alpha : (\hat{G}, \hat{H}, S) \xrightarrow{\sim} (\hat{\widetilde{G}}, \hat{\widetilde{H}}, \widetilde{S})$; if $\alpha(Y_0) = \widetilde{Y}_1$ and $\alpha(Y_1) = \widetilde{Y}_0$, composing $\alpha \times \alpha$ with the coordinate swap $\sigma(u, v) = (v, u)$ (which preserves $S$ via $(x, x^{-1}) \mapsto (x^{-1}, x)$) gives a folder isomorphism realizing $x \mapsto \alpha(x^{-1})$.
   - *($\Rightarrow$)*: By `C0001` Theorem 1.4(2), any loop isomorphism lifts to a folder isomorphism $\Phi : (\hat{G}, \hat{H}, S) \xrightarrow{\sim} (\hat{\widetilde{G}}, \hat{\widetilde{H}}, \widetilde{S})$. Then $\Phi(\operatorname{Soc}(\hat{G})) = \Phi(T \times T) = \widetilde{T} \times \widetilde{T}$, permuting the two unique minimal normal subgroups $T \times 1, 1 \times T$ onto $\widetilde{T} \times 1, 1 \times \widetilde{T}$. Pre-composing $\Phi$ with $\sigma$ if needed, we have $\Phi(T \times 1) = \widetilde{T} \times 1$ and $\Phi(1 \times T) = 1 \times \widetilde{T}$. Conjugation action of $(u, v) \in \hat{G}$ on $T \times 1$ (resp. $1 \times T$) depends solely on $u \in X \le \operatorname{Aut}(T)$ (resp. $v \in X \le \operatorname{Aut}(T)$) since $C_X(T) = 1$; thus $\Phi(u, v) = (\alpha_0(u), \alpha_1(v))$ on $\hat{G}$ for injective homomorphisms $\alpha_0, \alpha_1 : X \hookrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(\widetilde{T})$ (as $\operatorname{pr}_1(\hat{G}) = \operatorname{pr}_2(\hat{G}) = X$). Now $\Phi(S) = \widetilde{S}$ means that for every $x \in X$, $(\alpha_0(x), \alpha_1(x)^{-1}) = (w, w^{-1})$ for some $w \in \widetilde{X}$, forcing $\alpha_0(x) = w = \alpha_1(x)$ ($\forall x \in X$) and $\alpha_0(X) = \widetilde{X}$ (since $|S| = |\widetilde{S}|$). Thus $\alpha := \alpha_0 = \alpha_1 : X \xrightarrow{\sim} \widetilde{X}$ is a group isomorphism, and since $\operatorname{pr}_i(\hat{H}) = Y_i$ and $\operatorname{pr}_i(\hat{\widetilde{H}}) = \widetilde{Y}_i$, $\Phi(\hat{H}) = \hat{\widetilde{H}} \iff \alpha(Y_0) = \widetilde{Y}_0$ and $\alpha(Y_1) = \widetilde{Y}_1$.
   - *Automorphism group*: In particular, $\operatorname{Aut}(Q_{\mathcal{T}}) \cong \{\alpha \in \operatorname{Aut}(X) : \{\alpha(Y_0), \alpha(Y_1)\} = \{Y_0, Y_1\}\}$; whenever $|Y_0| \neq |Y_1|$ (which holds for every core-free exact factorization of an almost-simple group since $|Y_0||Y_1| = |X|$ and no triple below has $|Y_0| = |Y_1|$), $\operatorname{Aut}(Q_{\mathcal{T}}) \cong \operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{Aut}(X)}(Y_0) \cap \operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{Aut}(X)}(Y_1)$. $\square$

### 3. CFSG Classification of Core-Free Almost-Simple Triples $(X, Y_0, Y_1)$ Satisfying $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$

By Hering–Liebeck–Saxl (`HLS87`, zero core-free exact factorizations for exceptional Lie-type socles), Giudici (`Giu06`, Theorem 1.3), Burness–Li (`BL21`, Theorem 3 and Table 4, classifying all core-free exact factorizations with a solvable factor), Li–Wang–Xia (`LWX23`, Theorem 1.2 and Table 1.1, classifying all core-free exact factorizations with both factors nonsolvable), and Feng–Li–Li–Wang–Xia–Zou (`FLLWXZ25`, Theorems 1.2, 1.7, and 1.9, resolving the even-$q$ symplectic parity condition), we obtain the complete list of core-free exact factorizations of finite almost-simple groups $X$ ($\operatorname{Soc}(X) = T$) satisfying $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$ (equivalently $|T| = |X / T| \cdot |Y_0 \cap T| \cdot |Y_1 \cap T|$ with $T Y_i = X$; note that when $X / T$ is nonabelian so $T \lneq X' \lneq X$, $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$ is strictly stronger than the necessary condition $X = X' Y_0 = X' Y_1$). Up to swapping $(Y_0, Y_1)$, every such triple lies in one of **6 infinite families** or the **finite sporadic list** below:

#### Six Infinite Families ($X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$)
1. **$T = A_n$ ($n \ge 5$), point-stabilizer (`BL21` Thm 3(i), `LWX23` Table 1.1 Row 1)**:
   - $X = A_n$: $Y_1 = A_{n-1}$ and $Y_0 \le A_n$ any regular subgroup of order $n$ (for even $n$, $\operatorname{Syl}_2(Y_0)$ non-cyclic).
   - $X = S_n$ ($n = 2m \ge 6$ even): $Y_1 = S_{n-1}$ and $Y_0 \le S_n$ any regular subgroup of order $n$ with $Y_0 \not\le A_n$ (equivalently nontrivial cyclic $\operatorname{Syl}_2(Y_0)$, so $Y_0 = O_{2'}(Y_0) \rtimes C_{2^a}$ is solvable).
2. **$T = A_q$ ($q = p^a \ge 5$ prime power), $2$-homogeneous $1$-dimensional affine (`BL21` Thm 3(ii))**: $Y_0 \le \operatorname{A\Gamma L}_1(q)$ solvable $2$-homogeneous on $\mathbb{F}_q$ and $A_{q-2} \trianglelefteq Y_1 \le S_{q-2} \times S_2$ with $Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1$, either in $X = A_q$ ($Y_0, Y_1 \le A_q$) or in $X = S_q$ ($Y_0 \not\le A_q$ and $Y_1 \not\le A_q$).
3. **$T = A_{q+1}$ ($q \ge 7$ prime power), sharply $3$-transitive $\operatorname{PSL}_2(q)$-socle (`LWX23` Table 1.1 Row 2 / Ex. 4.1)**:
   - $X = A_{q+1}$: $Y_1 = A_{q-2}$ and $Y_0 = \operatorname{PSL}_2(q).\gcd(2, q-1) \le A_{q+1}$ sharply $3$-transitive ($q = 2^f \ge 8$, or $q = p^{2e}$ with $p^e \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ and $Y_0 = M(q+1)$).
   - $X = S_{q+1}$: $Y_0 = \operatorname{PSL}_2(q).2 \not\le A_{q+1}$ sharply $3$-transitive and $Y_1 = A_{q-2}.2 \not\le A_{q+1}$ with $Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1$ (e.g. $q \equiv 1 \pmod 4$, $Y_1 \in \{S_{q-2}, A_{q-2} \times C_2\}$).
4. **$T = A_{q+1}$ ($q \equiv 3 \pmod 4$, $q \ge 7$ prime power), $3$-homogeneous $\operatorname{PSL}_2(q)$ (`LWX23` Table 1.1 Row 3)**: $X = A_{q+1}$ only, $Y_0 = \operatorname{PSL}_2(q)$, $Y_1 = (S_{q-2} \times S_3) \cap A_{q+1} \cong A_{q-2}.2$.
5. **$T = \operatorname{PSL}_n(q)$ ($n \ge 2$, $(n, q) \notin \{(2, 2), (2, 3)\}$), $1$-dimensional semilinear $\times$ parabolic (`BL21` Thm 3(iii), `FLLWXZ25` Thm 1.7 & Rem. 3.2)**: $X \le \operatorname{P\Gamma L}_n(q)$ (no graph automorphism for $n \ge 3$), $Y_0 \cap T \le \frac{q^n - 1}{(q-1)\gcd(n, q-1)} : n$ solvable transitive on $\mathbb{P}^{n-1}(\mathbb{F}_q)$, and $q^{n-1} : \operatorname{SL}_{n-1}(q) \trianglelefteq Y_1 \cap T \le P_1(T)$ with $T Y_0 = T Y_1 = X$ (realized for every $(n, q) \notin \{(2, 2), (2, 3)\}$ by $X = \operatorname{PGL}_n(q)$, Singer cycle $Y_0 = C_{(q^n-1)/(q-1)}$, and $Y_1 = P_1(\operatorname{PGL}_n(q))$).
6. **$T = \operatorname{Sp}_{2m}(q)$ ($q = 2^f \ge 2$ even, $m \ge 3$ odd), parabolic-subfield $\times$ elliptic orthogonal (`BL21` Thm 3(iv), `FLLWXZ25` Thm 1.9)**: $X = \operatorname{Sp}_{2m}(q).C_r$ where $r \mid f$ is odd, $Y_1 = \Omega_{2m}^-(q).C_r$, and $Y_0 = q^m : S$ with $S \le \operatorname{\Gamma L}_1(q^m)$ regular on $\mathbb{F}_{q^m}^\times$ projecting onto $C_r$ (`FLLWXZ25` Theorem 1.9 proves zero core-free exact factorizations exist for even $m \ge 2$).
