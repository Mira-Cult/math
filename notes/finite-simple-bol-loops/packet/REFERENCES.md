## JS: simple Bol permutation rank

K. W. Johnson and J. D. H. Smith, *On the smallest simple, unipotent Bol loop*, *J. Combin. Theory Ser. A* **117**(7) (2010), 959–969, §§1.1, 2.1 ([author PDF](https://jdhsmith.math.iastate.edu/math/BolBoolf.pdf)); loop-character conjugacy definitions in K. W. Johnson and J. D. H. Smith, *Characters of finite quasigroups*, *European J. Combin.* **5**(1) (1984), 43–50, and K. W. Johnson, *Loop characters*, *Comment. Math. Univ. Carolin.* **41**(2) (2000), 271–281 (§2, p. 272 and §5(1), p. 278). The loop conjugacy classes of $(Q, 1)$ are the orbits of $\operatorname{Inn}(Q) = \operatorname{Mlt}(Q)_1$ on $Q$, so triviality of nontrivial loop conjugacy classes ($Q \setminus \{1\}$ a single class) is strictly equivalent to $2$-transitivity ($\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)) = 2$). Because Paige's simple Moufang loops $M(q)$ satisfy $\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Mlt}(M(q))) = q + 1 \ge 3$ for even $q$ and $\frac{q + 3}{2} \ge 3$ for odd $q$ (see C0009.md [context outside this selected packet: `claims/C0009.md`] and C0017.md [context outside this selected packet: `claims/C0017.md`]; cf. E. Bannai and S.-Y. Song, *Proc. London Math. Soc.* (3) **58**(2) (1989), 209–236), the rank-$2$ question `#0323/S1` strictly requires the **non-Moufang** hypothesis.
