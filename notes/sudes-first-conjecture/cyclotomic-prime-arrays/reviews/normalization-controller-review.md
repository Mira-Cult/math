## Elementary normalization and local arithmetic

The checked points are:

- Minkowski's quartic class bounds are below 2 for discriminants 125 and
  144 and below 3 for 256; the unique norm-two prime in K8 is principal.
  This proves class number one without relying on a field-table label.
- The displayed real quadratic units are fundamental. The conjugation
  quotient of a complex unit is torsion; the ramified valuation argument
  rules out the odd torsion class for K5 and K8. For K12, 1+zeta has
  conjugation quotient zeta and square equal to zeta times 2+sqrt(3).
  This proves the full unit group and the index-two difference from roots
  times real units, rather than assuming a convenient subgroup is full.
- The half-open logarithmic and angular intervals give one representative
  per entire associate class. Their comparisons reduce to exact real
  algebraic signs. The coefficient bounds give finite complete norm
  prefixes, and the stated norm/lex order handles incomplete last ties.
- The finite-field gcd certificate is sufficient and necessary: an
  irreducible gcd of degree f identifies a prime ideal of index p^f
  containing (alpha), and equality of indices gives equality of ideals.
  It includes higher residue degree and does not mistake an inert prime
  element for a composite merely because its rational norm is composite.
- The small-prime factorizations and F2/F4/F9/F16 distinctions are correct.
  Exact cumulative counts and the total are retained; an exceptional
  unramified input class is not silently removed from the prescribed pool.
- For K5 and K8 every integral boundary-ray element has positive odd
  valuation at the unique ramified prime. A retained prime is therefore
  strictly inside the sector. Every nonempty ordinary sum stays strictly
  inside it, excluding all ramified-prime associates, including those
  with arbitrarily large height. In K8 even-length sums are divisible
  by that prime, so cannot be prime or units. The proof does not make
  the invalid large-height-to-large-norm inference.
- The K12 limitation of the same angular obstruction is stated correctly.
  Its nonreal fundamental unit changes the first angle by half a torsion
  step. Unit/zero exclusion for arbitrary sums is not overclaimed.

The frozen minimal-height alternative is a genuinely different model.

all have compatible fixed-rank constants. Its seed gives an actual
integer lattice Gamma, not an assumed ideal. The determinant and adjugate
identity justify d_Gamma O contained in Gamma and the chosen ideal

coefficient bound pays the determinant: the factor d_Gamma in the final
residual cancels the inverse determinant in the seed adjugate. Doubled
directional multiplicities give an integral half-sum prefix center.

For Z4, the support-function bound gives real capacity in all four
coordinates. At an extreme point at most four coefficients are fractional.
Rounding them leaves a bounded residual in Gamma, which PF actually
realizes from reserved disjoint switches. The explicit full reference
is p0+q0. Complementary references differ in native action by O(log R),
and each whole pair is at its midpoint in expectation. No independent
coordinate permutation is introduced.

The integral-flow lemma and IT were checked separately from their tests.
After rounding the real tensor solution, the residual has size at most
Delta^2/2 and zero margins. Bounded integral column flows exist by cut
capacity. Subtracting their total from one column changes only a

second integral flow stage therefore exists. This gives the stated
linear loss, specifically 32(n-1) for degree 64 and expansion 8.
After multiplication by the seed basis, the additional O(n log R)
cost is o(K2 R) at the quartic cube scale. The old quadratic n^2

  exhaustive possible monic divisors through degree two;
