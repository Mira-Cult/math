# Exact cyclotomic array computation

There are two explicit models. Conductors 5,8,12 keep their frozen balanced-log
programs and certificates. The fixed-conductor continuation uses the energy
model for the new fields; its producer and independent checker are separate.
Use the format-specific globs below, since the JSON formats are intentionally
not interchangeable. Recorded replay runtime: CPython 3.12.14, standard library.

### Why the finite energy prefixes are certified

The exact LDL ellipsoid enumeration lists every coefficient vector with
E(alpha) at most the recorded integer bound. Prime elements are keyed by
their actual prime ideal `(p,g(zeta))`, where the norm is p^f and g is the
irreducible degree-f gcd modulo p. Among every such key the checker takes
the minimum energy and then the sector/lexicographic minimum. Every potentially
better associate has energy within the same enumerated ellipsoid, so this
is the global associate minimum, including every unit-index coset.

The four example fields' class numbers are independently certified as one by
Minkowski's ideal-class theorem and supplied generators for every prime ideal
below a rigorous upper bound. The recorded `bound_floor` is the floor of the
conservative rational upper bound

    (4/3)^(r/2) * (r!/r^r) * ceil(sqrt(abs(discriminant))),

using pi>3. It is not asserted to be the exact floor of the sharp Minkowski
constant. The bounds are 4,5,8,31 for c=7,9,15,16. Distinct verified prime-ideal
keys and the cyclotomic splitting count prove that every required small prime
ideal is represented, including ramified ideals in this class-number check.
Every ideal class consequently has a principal representative. The same key
count then proves completeness of all unramified prime ideals through each
array cutoff. Sorting settles its partial final tie exactly.

The finite array producer intentionally supports only c=7,9,15,16, whose
class-number-one completeness route is certified this way. It does not claim
to implement the general all-field enumeration algorithm in GN1. That theorem
instead uses a certified independent-unit bound and a complete coefficient
scan, so it requires no class-number-one assumption or principal-ideal oracle.

The c7 cube contains line primes of residue degrees 1,2,3,6; in particular its
inert prime target of degree 6 is retained. The c15 and c16 cubes also contain
degree-two prime targets. The checker rejects proper prime-ideal powers and
nonprime split rational elements, rather than rejecting every non-prime
rational norm.

Negative controls cover omitted classes and cutoff ties, insufficient energy
bounds, wrong field data, wrong Minkowski data, missing small prime ideals,
hidden unit rescaling, a coherent same-ideal associate with strictly greater
energy, repeated entries, wrong pool order, missing lines, and wrong prime
residue data. Boundary controls include the exact included and excluded rays
and large near-boundary perturbations.

### Independent units and the nonprincipal K23 control

`general_unit_certificates.py`, authored by /root/normalization, certifies
independent geometric-sum unit subgroups for c=7,9,11,15,16,20,24,23 using
outward rational intervals for pi, sine and logarithm, and a nonzero determinant
interval. The supplied input is `inputs/general_unit_subgroups.json`. These
are independent finite-index subgroups, not a claim that their index is one.
GN1 proves why their bounds suffice for exact full-associate normalization
and for a finite full-unit generating certificate. The utility is a bounded
explicit certificate tool, not the general dovetailed unit-search algorithm.

The same utility exactly checks the K23 quadratic Gauss sum and norm-descent
data. The proof shows that the two prime ideals above 2 are nonprincipal and
that the element 2 is irreducible but not prime. It does not claim the exact
class number of K23. No PARI, GRH-dependent unit output, or floating equality
test is used by these finite programs.

### Order-three cube obstruction and its tested K9 repair

For c=9, n=3, D=3, seed 20261103, energy bound 18, 100000 steps per restart
and fifteen restarts, the search exhausted its budget; its best arrangement
still had four nonprime required line sums. That search result proves no
impossibility. The separate [TC3 proof](../manuscripts/ternary_cube_obstruction.md)
now rules out this order at every conductor 3^a: all input residues modulo
the ramified prime above 3 are plus or minus one, and every two-coloring of
the full order-three cube has a monochromatic required line. A full-unit
sector argument excludes every exceptional ramified-prime associate from
that divisible line sum, regardless of its height.

The complete finite non-two-colorability proof is the 31-node tree
`inputs/ternary_cube3_unsat.json`. It records both children at every branch
and every forced assignment and conflict. The producer and author replay
are by /root/normalization; the independent checker is by /root and imports
neither author program. Both reconstruct all 49 required lines. The certificate
uses 47 of them, which already suffice for the obstruction. The independent
checker also offers a separate exhaustive search that does not read the
supplied proof tree.

```sh
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/check_ternary_cube_obstruction.py --negative-controls --independent-search problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/inputs/ternary_cube3_unsat.json
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/replay_ternary_cube_tree.py --negative-controls problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/inputs/ternary_cube3_unsat.json
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/generate_ternary_cube_obstruction.py /tmp/ternary_cube3_unsat.json
```

Independent replay verifies 31 nodes, 15 branches, 16 conflicting leaves,
220 forced assignments and maximum depth five, and rejects eight mutations.
Its separate search exhausts 15 nodes and eight conflicts. The same-author
replay passes two negative controls; it is an additional implementation,
not another independent reviewer. The supplementary review checks the
arithmetic reduction, certificate and separately authored checker.

The positive K9 order-four cube in the table uses exactly the first 64
canonical prime elements and has all 76 required line sums prime. Its
energy-24 ellipsoid contains 4951 points, accounting for every contributing
prime ideal through norm 307. This tests the next-order repair in the
unchanged K9 model; it does not establish order four for every power of three.

## Running code

Use Python 3.11 or newer, standard library only. From the repository root:

```sh
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/check_certificate.py --negative-controls problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/inputs/m*_square*.json problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/inputs/m*_cube*.json
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/tensor_rounding_check.py
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/vector_fixture.py verify problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/inputs/physical_*.json --negative-controls
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/local_audit.py verify problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/inputs/local_ledger.json --negative-controls
```

The independent array checker imports no producer functions. It verifies exact
norms by multiplication determinants, canonical boundaries by quadratic-surd
signs, finite quotient-field primality, prime-ideal uniqueness and completeness,
the cut norm tie, pool equality, all lines, and every prime line sum. The
producer uses a separate norm computation and independent line enumeration.
Prime norm is not substituted for prime element. No approximate embedding
decides membership.

## Reproducibility

`cyclotomic.py` supplies the exact producer. A coefficient-box scan stops only
after the found normalized prime ideals account for every unramified prime ideal
through the cutoff; their number is the sum of 4/ord_m(p) over p^ord_m(p)<=B.
This proves enumeration completeness without an approximate radius test.
`search_arrays.py` is seeded finite swap annealing. Its exhausted budget proves
nothing; each retained result is independently verified.

Each JSON records its seed, successful restart and step, complete norm cutoff,
all coefficients, and all required line and prime-ideal certificates. Reproduce
an example with the recorded budget, for instance:

```sh
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/search_arrays.py --m 5 --n 4 --steps 40000 --restarts 8 --output /tmp/m5_square4.json
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/search_arrays.py --m 8 --n 3 --D 3 --steps 100000 --restarts 20 --output /tmp/m8_cube3.json
```

At this source checkpoint the nine nontrivial retained arrays are squares
(m,n)=(5,3),(5,4),(8,3),(8,5),(12,3),(12,6), and all three full order-three
cubes. Their checker rejects 90 corrupted certificates. Eleven common exact
arithmetic controls cover zero, units, ramification, split rational composites,
and inert prime elements of non-prime rational norm. Those finite checks are
distinct from the eventual proof.

`tensor_rounding_check.py` independently recomputes the tensor incidence
identity for 192 bounded integer residuals at orders 3 through 18, seed
20261102, and rejects four adverse controls. It tests the new two-stage integer
flow correction, not prime distribution or the complete asymptotic construction.

## Four-direction physical fixtures

Each `physical_m*_cube9.json` uses exactly the first 729 canonical entries.
Four cuboid stages use four mutually disjoint equal-gap pairs each, with gaps
2e_0,2e_1,2e_2,2e_3 in the fixed integral basis. Their lattice has index 16.
All 16 stage orientations are actual permutations of the whole elements.
The verifier recomputes all 301 required line sums, preserves all axes and
the other three bodies, and checks every face-side-effect signature. These
are physical trade fixtures, expressly not all-prime-line arrays.

Their verification imports the independently authored arithmetic and line
enumeration from `check_certificate.py`; it does not trust producer norms,
declared gap independence, or input-pool membership. Ten corruptions per
field are rejected, including a dependent gap basis, wrong lattice index,
wrong residue, shared endpoints, fewer than four equal-gap pairs, missing
lines, missing orientations, hidden unit rescaling, and incomplete prime pools.

Reproduce each with, for example:

```sh
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/vector_fixture.py generate --m 5 --output /tmp/physical_m5_cube9.json
```

## Local ledgers and finite obstructions

`local_audit.py` records and verifies 21 complete initial prefixes, with
49 cumulative residue histograms and their exact coefficient totals. It
includes the zero residue of the retained inert element 2 in K5 modulo
the unramified ideal (2), and degree-two prime ideals at 3 in K8. These
classes are retained rather than silently treating all inputs as units.

At order two every pair of cells is a required line, both in the square
and full cube. Each field's first two canonical entries have a nonprime
sum, with norm 155, 4, or 225 for m=5,8,12. The ledger's exact quotient
tests certify these sums as nonprime, proving all six order-two instances
impossible. Five corruptions of totals, counts, cutoff completeness, and
obstruction witnesses are rejected. No failed search is used as an obstruction.

```sh
python -B problems/cyclotomic-prime-arrays/code/local_audit.py generate /tmp/local_ledger.json --negative-controls
```
