# Open obligations and relationships

## Established equivalence classes

Finite bijection certificates are equivalent only to the specified finite
instances. No eventual theorem follows from a finite collection of examples.

## One-way implications

[PC4](manuscripts/controller.md) and [W4,T4,S4](manuscripts/analytic.md),
with the normalization/local lemmas, imply [CPA4](manuscripts/composition.md).
Real-span coverage alone does not imply bounded integer attainability or
physical control; the reserved prefix and actual pairs supply that distinction.

For the extended canonical family, [GN1--GN3](manuscripts/general_normalization_local.md),
[GW,GP,GE,GT,GS](manuscripts/general_analytic.md), and
[GC(r)](manuscripts/general_controller.md) imply
[CYC](manuscripts/general_composition.md) for every fixed conductor.
These include the required arithmetic, principal-prime and geometry audits;
class number one is not an unresolved premise.

## Conditional reductions

No unresolved mathematical premise is retained in the concrete author proof
beyond the explicitly cited established analytic and pinned geometric results.
Independent review, kernel verification, and integration remain separate.

The abstract fixed-number-field transfer [GC(r)](manuscripts/general_controller.md)
has explicit density, boundedness, centered-endpoint, prime-box, and norm-two
coset hypotheses. They are now verified for the extended cyclotomic family.
Applying it to a different field or a different normalization still requires
independent proofs of those hypotheses.

## Conjectured links

Minimum coefficient height, minimum trace energy, and balanced-log canonical
pools need not have the same array sums. No equivalence is asserted. The
energy domain has its own proved geometric theory. Exact face decisions for
an arbitrary higher-rank logarithmic parallelotope are not supplied by that
theory or by the earlier rank-one logarithmic argument.

## Ungrouped questions

1. Determine finite exceptional admissible orders; side two is completely
   excluded in the three frozen quartic fields and both dimensions. The
   supplemental [TC3](manuscripts/ternary_cube_obstruction.md) excludes
   order-three full cubes at every conductor 3^a. The unchanged K9 model
   has a certified order-four full cube. Other finite exceptions and
   positive order-four results at other powers of three remain open here.
2. Develop minimum coefficient height geometrically, or an exact boundary
   algorithm for arbitrary higher-rank logarithmic parallelotopes.
3. Obtain effective numerical thresholds and an efficient construction.
4. Extend the physical composition beyond squares and full cubes while paying
   for both arithmetic rank and spatial dimension. The other spatial-dimension
   lanes remain separate owned workspaces.
5. Study conductor growth with the array order. The fixed-field theorem gives
   no uniform threshold or growing-degree estimates.

The quartic reviews remain complete in their original scopes; reviews of the
new general scientific source are recorded separately in [the handoff](RECOVERY.md).
