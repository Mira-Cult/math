# Provenance, review, and verification limits

## Scientific scope

This packet presents the proposed unconditional conventional theorem that
canonical Gaussian prime squares and full signed-line cubes exist at every
sufficiently large odd order, and their Eisenstein counterparts at every
sufficiently large integer order. The exact canonical pools include inert
prime elements and norm-tie truncations. Thresholds are ineffective and
unevaluated. Finite exceptions are not claimed completely classified.

## Review status

The source delivery records separate nonauthor analytic/local-arithmetic and
physical-controller mathematical reviews, plus implementation and physical-trade
reviews. For publication, two further bounded-scope nonauthor reviews examined
(1) the pinned primary analytic statements, local factors, exact-total targets,
and residue arithmetic, and (2) the rank-two physical controller, integer image,
even-order geometry, and independently replayed finite evidence. Neither found
a blocking defect within its stated scope.

These are internal research reviews. They are not external peer acceptance,
proof-assistant certification, or an independent reproving of the cited
analytic literature. Conventional proof correctness remains distinct from
successful finite verification. No literature-priority claim is made.

## Publication-side finite replay

The independently reconstructed finite checker verified:

- Ten successful array certificates: Gaussian squares 1, 3, 5 and cubes 1, 3;
  Eisenstein squares 1, 3, 5, 6 and cube 1.
- Three complete residue-obstruction trees: Eisenstein squares 2 and 4, and
  the full signed order-three cube. The cube tree proves that every two-coloring
  of the **27 cells** has a monochromatic one of the 49 required lines.
- Rejection of 84 corrupted array/obstruction certificates and 14 arithmetic
  controls; 3,049 local-arithmetic controls. An additional direct ring-divisor
  comparison checked 1,250 elements, and 1,332 even-order face-cuboid incidence
  checks passed at orders 10 and 12.
- Two order-19 physical fixtures, each using 6,859 prescribed entries, two
  independent gap directions and four legal orientations, checked against
  all 1,201 signed lines. Twenty corrupted fixtures were rejected.
- Byte-identical regeneration of the six nontrivial successful search outputs
  and both physical fixtures; all three regenerated obstruction trees matched
  as JSON values (formatting is not part of that latter claim).

The physical fixtures are legal whole-element vector trades, not examples in
which all line sums are prime. Finite controls do not prove infinite prime
supplies, asymptotic capacities, or the eventual theorem. The proof of those
claims is the conventional manuscript chain.
