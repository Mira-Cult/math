# Supporting proofs for explicit full signed-line prime cubes

[Manuscript V](../explicit-prime-cube.md) proves the result for every odd
$`n\ge2^{61750}`$, with the exact first $`n^3`$ odd primes and all
$`3n^2+6n+4`$ full signed-coordinate line sums prime. This is an entire
odd-order tail, not a subsequence. Target sums may repeat; entries may not.
No whole-square-section primality requirement is imposed.

## Complete new components

- [Effective logarithmic prime windows](explicit_signed_analytic.md): signed exceptional-zero terms, primitive-character normalization, progression lifting, exact-pool mean, numerical domains and real-tail inequalities. The fixed-denominator extension needed by the final constants is proved in Section 2 of the main manuscript.
- [Finite signed-cube controller](explicit_signed_controller.md): explicit packing, occupancy, whole-reservoir centering, grid floors and successive corrections. Its original prime-supply contract is replaced, not asserted unchanged, in the final composition.
- [Weighted controller envelopes](explicit_signed_weighted_controller.md): rational coefficients, pointwise ceilings and the constant-last-scale physical interface. Its earlier profiles are component comparisons, not additional complete cube cutoffs.
- [Phased allocation and reflected bins](explicit_signed_reflected_supplies.md): the replacement prime-supply proof, with early deletion invoices and adaptive aggregate matching across disjoint bins and classes.
- [Finite TQ targets and the complete cube composition](explicit_signed_targets_tq.md): exact totals, weighted off-diagonal orbits, exactly $`n`$ finishing slots, six face-family lists and four body targets; the sharp target radius is retained.
- [Earlier finite logarithmic-window target interface](explicit_signed_targets.md): the complete FL-based predecessor, preserved for comparison and its common residue bookkeeping. The final bound uses TQ, not the older FL window.

## Already-public finite and analytic dependencies

The [signed incidence](../cube/signed_incidence.md), [physical trades](../cube/signed_trades.md), and [integer tensor routing](../cube/incidence.md) are reused. The degree-64 graph and complete-grid arguments are in the [square controller](../controller.md); the [finite target-list proof](../explicit-targets.md) supplies the predecessor FL interface.

The effective inputs reuse [classical prime estimates](../classical-primes.md), especially the corrected primitive-character proof E2, and the following complete components of Manuscript III: [totient normalization](../optimized/cutoff_analytic_optimization.md), [signed endpoint errors](../optimized/cutoff_analytic_refinement.md), [linear-window estimates](../optimized/cutoff_linear_window_analytic.md), [parameterized zero sums](../optimized/cutoff_relaxed_height_analytic.md), and [original integral factors and exceptional signs](../optimized/cutoff_constant_window_analytic.md). Their actual hypotheses are specialized in the new analytic manuscript; neither the square cutoff nor the square controller parameters are simply applied to cubes.

These proof dependencies are all public. The canonical research archive's executable recovery and numerical checks are described in [provenance](../PROVENANCE.md#explicit-prime-cube); this directory is the readable proof edition, not a relocated executable archive.
