# Explicit external imports

1. L. Lu and L. A. Székely, *Using Lovász Local Lemma in the Space of Random Injections*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics **14**(1) (2007), R63. [Journal / DOI 10.37236/981](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v14i1r63), [author PDF](https://people.math.sc.edu/lu/papers/randinj.pdf). The permutation bridge uses the negative-dependency theorem (Theorem 1, printed p. 4) and the lopsided local lemma (Lemma 3, printed p. 2). The author PDF's volume-13 footer is a typo; the journal identifies volume 14.

2. J. S. Milne, *A Primer of Commutative Algebra*, v4.03 (23 March 2020), §4, Propositions 4.5–4.7, pp. 16–17. [Author PDF](https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/CA.pdf). Used for Gauss's lemma and primitive factorization.

All special height counts, density estimates, Fourier bounds, conditional sampling estimates, reducibility-window counts, and cutoff inequalities are proved in the included manuscripts. No hypothesis about prime patterns, unproved polynomial equidistribution, or the resolved integer-prime square conjecture is imported. All logarithms in proofs are natural except explicitly base-two logarithms.
