# Horner/diagonal frontier: source coverage and compiler preservation

## Status and scope

This is a producer-authored conventional proof for `machines/horn-horner/source.py`
and the `frontier/build.py` pipeline. It is not independent review, a kernel
certificate, or a lower bound. Its dependencies are the unchanged Horn capture,
one-relation normalization and finite-graph conventions in `horn-reduction.md`,
and the unchanged unary-register physical simulation in `COMPILER_PROOF.md`.
The separate new compiler tests and literal tape observations are evidence for
implementations, not substitutes for the unbounded arguments below.

The table is total, binary, single-tape, initially blank, with a two-way infinite
tape and only left/right moves. Its header specifies start state 0, B=1 and head
0. Transition t>=1 emits exactly when its pretransition state is B. No formal
axiom about P versus NP or about a proof system's completeness for P=NP is used.

## 1. Changed candidate enumeration

Candidates are pairs (k,p), k>=2 and p>=0. Start at (2,0). After a rejection,
replace (2,p) by (p+3,0), and replace (k,p), k>2, by (k-1,p+1).
Put s=k+p-2 and

    rank(k,p) = s(s+1)/2 + p.

For k>2 the successor keeps s and increases p by one. At k=2, p=s;
its rank is the last rank on diagonal s and its successor is the first on
s+1. Thus the rank increases by exactly one. Conversely every nonnegative
rank has a unique diagonal and an offset 0<=p<=s, giving k=s-p+2.
The enumeration is a bijection and uses unbounded naturals, not a fixed-width
counter. No search cap occurs in the executable specification.

This changes candidate order relative to the 783-state machine. Equality of
its physical beep times, least successful index, or total finite beep count
with the old machine is NOT claimed. The target theorem is the same.

## 2. Horner atom encoding and soundness

Retain the previous radix 8(k+2)^k+1, the signed atom kinds and the convention
that only the first literal of a clause can be a positive guessed-relation
atom. All codes therefore denote Horn clauses, including codes whose signs
would otherwise have made later guessed-relation atoms positive.

Let the term-word's nonzero-length base-(k+2) expansion, least significant
digit first, be t_0,...,t_(r-1); r<=k. The old evaluator built an index using
place weights. The new evaluator starts at index=0 and applies

    index <- n*index + value(t_i).

Consequently its little-endian tuple is
(value(t_(r-1)),...,value(t_0),0,...,0). The zero word is the all-zero tuple.
The source reference `frontier/reference.py` constructs this tuple explicitly;
it does not duplicate the arithmetic evaluation loop.

A fixed candidate remains a fixed existential second-order Horn sentence on
finite successor graphs. Its k-ary guessed relation has n^k Boolean entries;
grounding the fixed clauses gives only polynomially many Horn variables and
clauses as a function of the input graph size. Ordinary Horn propagation decides
its truth in polynomial time. The research machine instead enumerates all
relation interpretations. That implementation is finite but need not be
polynomial, and is not the reason for candidate soundness.

## 3. Coverage despite loss of leading zero term digits

A Horner word directly represents any k-tuple of term *codes* whose first
coordinate is nonzero, and also the all-zero tuple. This restriction cannot
just be ignored. The following explicit guarded translation restores coverage
of every sentence in the old candidate language.

For an old k-ary candidate, add a new universally quantified variable z=x_k and
use new arity k'=k+1. Its term code is k+2. In every original atom replace every
occurrence of the first-element term (code zero) by z, and append z as the last
tuple coordinate. Add the input literal NOT(z=first) at the END of every clause.
The guessed relation is now R'(x_0,...,x_(k-1),z). The extra coordinates of edge,
successor and equality atoms are ignored, as specified by the original engine.

All transformed atoms have a nonzero first term code. The guard's first term
is also z. Thus reverse all k' term codes to obtain a base-(k'+2) term word with
nonzero highest digit: the new evaluator reads exactly the desired full tuple.
The guard is extensional and appended, so the original head remains first and
the Horn restriction is preserved. An empty clause becomes just the guard;
an empty conjunction remains empty.

If z differs from the first element, the guard is true and imposes no condition.
At z=first, the guard is false, every replacement of zero by z has its old
meaning, and the slice R'(-,first) must satisfy exactly the old clauses. Hence
an interpretation of R' exists iff an interpretation of the old R exists.
An old model extends arbitrarily to other slices; a new model restricts to the
old one. This proves coverage for every n>=2, not just the tested finite cases.
The existing mixed-arity/nullary normalization composes with this translation.

The reference construction `lift` implements this map. Its tests compare
truth by independently grounded Horn closure; their finite number is not the
coverage proof.

## 4. Total comparison, progress and eventual silence

For each candidate the source enumerates n=2,3,... and all n*n-bit directed
edge matrices in increasing integer order. Loops and self-loops are allowed.
This is the same NP-complete graph language and input convention as the
preserved baseline. A new arity k is fixed during this whole graph scan.

For each graph, the evaluator tries at most 2^(n^k) relation interpretations,
at most n^k variable assignments per interpretation, and finitely many code
digits per clause. All nested divisions have positive divisors. Horner term
scans strictly decrease a finite natural. The independent coloring routine
tries at most 3^n color assignments and finitely many edge bits. Every graph
comparison terminates for arbitrarily large n,k,p.

By Sections 2-3 and the inherited capture theorem, a candidate agrees with
three-colorability on all graphs iff it defines that property, and some such
candidate exists iff P=NP. Every unsuccessful candidate has a finite-index
graph counterexample. The sequential controller reaches it, emits one beep,
advances the pair, and restarts the graph enumeration.

If P!=NP, every candidate is rejected and infinitely many beeps occur. If P=NP,
the least successful diagonal rank j is reached after finitely many terminating
tests; thereafter every finite graph index is reached in finite time without
another beep. Including the initial beep, the finite count is 1+j. Both cases
continue running. This is quasihalting/eventual silence, not ordinary halting,
and no finite quiet trace identifies the successful candidate.

## 5. Shared radix-digit kernel

The generic compiler recognizes pure, call-free expressions

    (a // (b**d)) % b.

With A=a, B=b and D=d, it performs D+1 divisions. Before each division, Q=R=C=0.
After a division by positive B, Q=floor(A/B), R=A mod B, A=C=0, and B is unchanged.
If D>0, it clears R, moves Q to A, decrements D and repeats. Otherwise it clears
Q and B and returns R. Induction gives the requested radix digit.

The generic zero-divisor convention is also preserved: quotient by zero is zero
and remainder is the dividend. At b=0,d=0 the expression is a (0**0=1); at b=0,
d>0 it is zero. The generic loop has exactly these outcomes. All iterations
terminate, including base zero and one. The extra index register is zero on
return, as are all shared arithmetic cells other than the returned result.

The recognized term expression

    (n*(n-1+n*a) // (n**t)) % n

has digits 0,n-1,a_0,a_1,... for n>=1. It therefore returns zero at t=0, n-1 at
t=1, and the (t-2)-nd digit of a otherwise. Saturating natural subtraction makes
the original expression zero when n=0; the generic kernel includes that case.
Calls inside either matched expression disable the optimization, because
repeated evaluation of a base could emit events. Equal syntax alone is not a
license to delete effects.

## 6. Checked positive-divisor specialization

`accumulator/positive.py` establishes a sufficient static precondition rather
than accepting a caller-supplied assertion. It starts with no positive variable
invariants. Parameters and variables that may be read before assignment cannot
receive an invariant. A new lower bound is admitted only when every assignment
has that lower bound using previously established invariants, and every update
preserves it. Addition of naturals and multiplication by an expression bounded
below by one preserve the bound. Unsupported/destructive updates prevent it.

Bounds propagate through natural constants, addition, multiplication, safe
constant subtraction, positive-base powers, and division by positive constants.
All ordinary AND augmented divisions/remainders must have a proved divisor
bound at least one. The proof is induction over the finite order in which
invariants were admitted, followed by induction over source execution. It also
covers loop iterations; it does not infer that a loop executes.

For the selected source, n>=2 and the encoded clause radix is positive. Removing
the zero-divisor arms therefore preserves its mathematical execution. The
positive term kernel is safe because its base is also a checked modulus divisor.
The complete generic fallback table (716 states) is preserved separately.
Nine adversarial source mutations are rejected; these tests supplement rather
than prove this abstract argument.

## 7. Consuming dead reads and direct guards

The backwards analysis unions branch successors and solves each loop's finite
variable-set liveness equation to a fixed point. Break and continue have explicit
successors. A read can be moved rather than copied only when its source cell is
dead afterward. Unknown/effectful expression forms are treated conservatively.

Operand(Name) can defer a load until other operand computations have finished.
Accordingly the implementation further excludes a name occurring more than
once anywhere in a simultaneous expression group, including a tuple assignment
and an augmented assignment's implicit left read. This prevents a nested last
read from consuming a value required by an earlier deferred reference. Values
live across calls, branches, loop backedges and candidate updates remain intact.
The guarantee concerns source-live values, not stale dead storage.

A direct variable guard uses DECNZ followed immediately by INC in the nonzero
case before entering the body. Thus it tests the source value without copying
it and restores it before either successor; zero remains zero. Loop and
inverted-guard branches use the same invariant and the resolved lexical
break/continue semantics. No arithmetic dictionary register remains live across
the body solely to hold a guard.

## 8. Physical compilation and event preservation

The new compiler emits only the unchanged INC/DECNZ/BEEP and structured-control
primitives. Closure, assignment/reset requirements, frame mapping, maximum
control-path depth and static register count are checked for each selected
build. The 26 registers fit inside the counted initializer's 1,909 empty slots;
this bounds their NUMBER, never their natural values or tape use. Register
operations shift the finite occupied suffix as needed and remain valid for
arbitrarily large values.

The inherited simulation theorem therefore maps every finite source operation
to finitely many physical transitions and every source beep to exactly one exit
from B. The colored quotient is checked row by row. Totalization sends only
invalid/unreachable physical branches to a silent sink. All those states,
including startup and B, are counted.

At source events the live candidate pair is compared with the physical run.
Discarded graph/evaluator scratch is not falsely advertised as source-observable.
Independently, EVERY physical register, the entire tape, state and head agree
with the separate primitive interpreter and two literal simulators at the
checked events. This is a stronger implementation check than matching beep
counts alone, but still finite evidence.

The result is a complete locally checked 707-state table with the stated
unbounded conventional proof. Independent review of this proof and the frozen
implementation remains outstanding. The older tables and their event bindings
are preserved; no Diophantine representation is changed or claimed here.
