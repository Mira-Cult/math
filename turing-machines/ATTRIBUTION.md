# Turing-machine attribution

Attribution travels with each machine package:

- [12-state Collatz evaluator](collatz/evaluator12/ATTRIBUTION.md): Pascal Michel's construction and shared arithmetic influences.
- [16-state Collatz cycle detector](collatz/cycle16/ATTRIBUTION.md): Charlie Reams's search strategy and the explicitly identified proof adaptation.
- [17-state Collatz beeper](collatz/beeping17/ATTRIBUTION.md): the 14-state arithmetic lineage and the public release's ordinary proof.

The complete earlier Collatz attribution text is retained in the
[family attribution](collatz/ATTRIBUTION.md). New machine families carry their
own provenance and attribution inside their package.
