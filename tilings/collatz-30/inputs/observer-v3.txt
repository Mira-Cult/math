Use the specified 30 polygons by translations and rotations, without reflections,
with disjoint interiors and union R^2. Edge-to-edge placement is not required.
Let u,v be perpendicular unit vectors with det(u,v)=1. A corner Q(a,b;p,u,v)
means one tile has consecutive genuine sides with vertices p-a*v, p, p-b*u
in counterclockwise boundary order, with interior angle pi/2 at p. Genuine
means a full maximal straight side, including both endpoints. A disk of radius
1+max(a,b) about p suffices to inspect the two sides and their maximality.
ROOT(p,u,v) is Q(38,43;p,u,v); its two sides need not share a neighbor.
EVENT(p,u,v) is Q(32,13;p,u,v), with one other tile sharing both relative side
interiors. The two predicate radii are 44 and 33. Colors and labels are unused.
R(tau) iff there exist p,u,v as above such that ROOT(p,u,v) and, for every
integer k>=1, there exists an integer j>=k such that
EVENT(p+13*u+(66*j-34)*v,u,v).
The root and oriented frame are chosen before k. The fixed offset selects that
root's spine. The observer reads no bits, carry states, integer values or paths.
