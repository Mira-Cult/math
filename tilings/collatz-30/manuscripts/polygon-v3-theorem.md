# Thirty fourteen-sided polygons with grounded recurrence for full Collatz

## Statement, model and quantifiers

The canonical geometric model is in [PROBLEM.md](../PROBLEM.md). Let P be the
thirty explicit polygons specified below. A legal completed tiling uses copies
of P by translations and arbitrary rotations, without reflections, with disjoint
interiors and union equal to the entire plane. Edge-to-edge placement is **not**
assumed. Nothing is preplaced. In particular no infinite boundary, marked seed,
coordinate origin or free initial integer is part of the instance.

The fixed geometric predicates ROOT and EVENT, and the global recurrence R,
are defined in the [literal observer specification](../inputs/observer-v3.txt).
They inspect two consecutive genuine straight sides each. In an oriented unit
frame (u,v), ROOT has consecutive incoming/outgoing side lengths (38,43),
and EVENT has lengths (32,13). The incoming direction is v and the outgoing
direction is -u, with a convex right angle. Both EVENT sides share another
tile; ROOT does not impose that extra requirement. Their bounded patch radii
are 44 and 33 respectively.

The entire acceptance formula is

    R(tau) iff there exist p,u,v with det(u,v)=1 and u,v orthonormal,
        ROOT(p,u,v), and
        for every integer k>=1 there exists an integer j>=k such that
            EVENT(p+13*u+(66*j-34)*v,u,v).

The same root and frame are selected before the universal threshold. An
arbitrary infinite spatial collection of event-like corners is insufficient.
No digit decoding, arithmetic interpreter or computation of Collatz is part
of either local predicate or of R.

**Theorem (ordinary mathematical proof).**

    [For every positive n, some ordinary Collatz iterate of n is 1]
        iff
    [There exists a completed legal plane tiling tau by P with R(tau)].

This is Track B, recurrent tiling existence. Ordinary tileability of P is
unconditional, even with an intrinsic root. Track A remains unresolved. The
proof does not decide Collatz, prove a global minimum, or classify the general
unrestricted recurrent-tiling existence problem. Verification scope is recorded in [the public validation guide](../VALIDATION.md);
no proof-assistant certificate or external human expert review is claimed.

## Geometric compiler and local observer correctness

The [geometric proof](geometry-v3.md) proves forward whole-plane placement
and its global converse for the actual fourteen-side family. Its exact finite
certificate handles all possible reflex-corner fillers, including rotated
copies and partial-edge contacts. Some alternatives require joint constraints
on two different corners; others are rejected by a bounded unfillable cavity.
The cavity proof uses closed mandatory cores, a connected tile interior,
and an area smaller than every tile, not a floating-point packing picture.
The finite certificate is checked independently of the producer's flood fill.

That argument forces one common orientation and a translated 66Z² grid,
then matching source colors and no extra tiles. All geometric tilings therefore
decode to legal source arrays, up to one harmless global translation and
rotation. Reflected computations, shifted phases, independent rotations and
fractional-edge bypasses are not further accepted cases. They are excluded
by the compiler. Its proof does not assume the existence of a root.

Literal inspection of every convex corner of every polygon gives ROOT only
on type29, at its carrier-local point (66,66). EVENT occurs at (79,32) on
types6 and27. Above ROOT in its own source column, the frame forces only
spine types, so EVENT identifies exactly RESET spine27 there. Consequently
its row-j anchor is precisely p+13u+(66j-34)v. The observer's contextual
restriction is the displayed fixed affine offset, not hidden source decoding.
The bounded patches include both entire genuine sides and neighborhoods of
their endpoints. Colors and the illustrative blue/gold/red names are not rules.

## What every legal tiling decodes

The [source-frame theorem](stream-frame-v3.md) proves these statements for
every decoded completed source array:

1. A ROOT is unique if present. It forces its entire upward spine and every
   rightward arithmetic row. By inspecting the row immediately above ROOT,
   geometry forces the initial right-infinite row to be the all-zero pair
   stream. ROOT supplies parity1. This is exactly saved target1, active1.
2. Every rooted row obeys the literal carry equations on every binary prefix.
   The spine synchronizes active parity. The saved odd target is always a
   standard positive integer, copied except at full-row RESET, when it advances
   by exactly2. No input is skipped or substituted.
3. Rootless tilings and arbitrary exterior components are permitted. They
   cannot satisfy this R without an actual root or alter the forced quadrant.
   Infinite backward histories do not replace its grounded initial row.
4. A legal active stream need not be a finite-support ordinary integer.
   Such nonstandard runs are explicitly classified nonaccepting exceptions:
   once nonstandard, the run has no later RESET. All arithmetic rows in an
   accepted root's quadrant have finite support and represent positive integers.

The fourth item is a deliberate change from the finite-frontier v2 contract.
It is not a claim that all legal tilings are canonical or standard. The proof
uses a finite future reset to establish standardness of earlier rows. RESET
compares the entire active stream with the standard saved target, so its input
is standard. Working backward through a finite segment, DOUBLE and PREIMAGE
force standard positive predecessors. Under R every row precedes such a reset.
The telescoping carry equations then force a genuine zero final carry beyond
the finite support; the remaining row is its fixed zero self-loop. There is
no phantom carry, unfinished reset or infinite-duration arithmetic core in an
accepted tiling. No finite end marker or unique cutoff is asserted.

## Accepted tiling implies full positive Collatz

Let a legal completed tiling satisfy R. Decode it using the compiler. ROOT
gives the unique initialized sequence above, and every counted event is a
full RESET of that same sequence. The [arithmetic proof](arithmetic-v3.md)
shows that the finite positive values between resets obey exactly

    (n,v) -> (n,2v),
    (n,3w+1) -> (n,w), where w is positive and odd,
    (n,n) -> (n+2,1), emitting one event.

Thus each finite block begins at active1 and ends at the current saved odd
target n. Every nonreset step reverses a genuine ordinary Collatz edge.
Reading the block backward gives a finite positive trajectory from n to1.
The target begins at1 and advances by exactly2. Unbounded event times give
infinitely many such blocks and cover every positive odd target. Every positive
integer reaches its odd part by finitely many halvings; hence full Collatz.

Equivalently, if a least failing odd target b exists, no finite block from1
can certify it. Any rooted legal run has at most (b-1)/2 events and cannot
pass R. This applies whether the failure is a nontrivial cycle or a nonperiodic
nonreturn. Exceeding the starting value does not stop or invalidate a trajectory.
One early event copied spatially, a bounded prefix repeated infinitely often,
or a nonstandard silent component cannot supply unbounded logical event times
on the same unique root's spine.

## Full Collatz implies an accepted completed tiling

Assume full Collatz. For n=1,3,5,..., take its finite ordinary trajectory to
its first1 and reverse that finite sequence. The two nonreset rules above
realize it; then use RESET. Concatenating the blocks gives an infinite rooted
row sequence with an event for every target. Each block is finite, including
its reset. The literal carry rules realize every finite standard relation,
with arbitrary zero padding. The width needed at a fixed logical row is finite
and can grow without bound over time.

Place ROOT at source site(0,0), its spine above, and every complete arithmetic
row to the right. Place BG everywhere with x<0 or y<0, and throughout the
initial zero row to the right of ROOT. The source-frame proof checks all
interfaces, including every exterior site. The compiler places each polygon
at the corresponding 66Z² site and proves full plane coverage and disjoint
interiors. This produces a completed tiling satisfying R, not just arbitrarily
large computation patches or an iterative assembly process.

The conditional accepted constructor is separate from the finite polygon
generator and the observer. Its totality follows under the stated Collatz
assumption. Without that assumption the all-BG constructor and quiet doubling
rooted constructor are still total and give ordinary tilings. The theorem
therefore has no vacuous universal-acceptance reading.

## Cost, variants and evidence limits

The expanded polygons, generator/dependency bytes, exact coordinate size, both
bounded local predicates and the global affine recurrence are separate costs.
This public package selects only the 30-shape construction. The selected set
uses no extra matching labels. A matching-rule presentation would be another
model, not a one-polygon improvement.

The same forward construction uses translations only, so the equivalence also
holds in that restricted model. No reflection-allowed converse or edge-to-edge
variant is established. The known syntactic hierarchy observation about finite
Wang tileability does not rule out a Collatz-specific Track A result and is not
used as an impossibility theorem here.

Exact validators, malformed certificates and recurrence adversaries are
listed in [the public validation guide](../VALIDATION.md).
Finite computations discharge explicitly finite implementation obligations;
they do not certify recurrence or decide Collatz. The poster is a presentation
of the exact motifs and one finite valid window. Its two illustrated events
are not evidence that the infinite recurrence has been realized.
